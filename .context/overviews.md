# Ongoing-story overviews (the `link_overviews` step, built and switched off)

An **overview** follows one ongoing development (an outbreak, a war, a negotiation, a rule-making) across our stories: a charter saying what belongs, a title, and a timeline of entries, one per story. The **link step** files each newly published story under the development it reports on, or starts a development when two stories report the same one. Plan: `.plans/2026-11-18_overviews-linking.md`; decision: ADR-0070.

**What is built and what it does today.** Phases 1 to 3 of the plan: the records, the answer keys' version 2 and the halves; linking (candidates, the link confirmation, founding, roles); the upkeep after linking (end-event closing, the status machine, bursts, reviews and the monthly audit, "Where this stands"); the `link_overviews` job after publishing, the decisions log, and the evals of each part in an in-memory sandbox. **Nothing runs in production:** the job row is seeded disabled, enabling it is refused while its eval gate is closed and while research passes' is, and nothing outside the job reads the tables yet. Phases 4 to 6 (the admin views and editor actions; the public page and story field; the switch) are still to come.

## Records

Migration `20261118203742_overviews`, additive. Only `services/overviewLifecycle.ts` writes these four tables (`test/overviewWriters.test.ts` fails on a Prisma write or raw INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE anywhere else outside `scripts/` and `test/`).

- **`overviews`**: title, `slug` (null until the overview is public, phase 5), `issue_id` (the founding story's issue), `scope` (`single`, `forming`, `ongoing`; a founding makes `forming`), `status` (`open`, `quiet`, `closing`, `closed`), `charter` (`{ in, out[], endEvent }`, with the model and prompt version that wrote it), `frozen`, `hidden`, `founded_at` (the earlier founding story's publication date) and `last_entry_at` (the newest entry's), so both are in the development's time, not the run's; `closing_since`, `closed_at` and `closed_reason` (`end_event`, `faded`, `editor`), and `entries_at_last_review` and `reviewed_at` (the reviews' triggers).
- **`overview_entries`**: one per story and overview, `kind` `story` (a CHECK requires `story_id` for it), `role` (`turning_point`, `routine`) and `role_kind` (a turning point's kind: setback, response, spread, decision, escalation, deescalation, money, science, people, milestone), `status` (`active`, `detached`), `judgment`, who joined it, `locked` (an editor's detach: automation never links the story there again), `shared` (the judgment record: `{ cause, place, end }`, each `{ story, other }` quoting this entry's story and the other side), the confidence, model and prompt version, and the decision that made it. **A story has at most one active entry** (partial unique index `overview_entries_one_active`).
- **`overview_stands`**: the versions of "Where this stands", numbered per overview; **one current at most** (`overview_stands_one_current`); `sentences` and `doing` (`[{ text, facts: [pool fact id] }]`), `facts_seen` (the facts offered), `as_of` (the day it was written for), model, prompt version and the checks' record.
- **`overview_decisions`**: every automated or editor decision (`kind` `link`, `found`, `role`, `stands`, `status`, `close`, `review`, the editor's actions, `undo`), with its outcome, inputs, the model's answer, model, prompt version, confidence, cost and actor; `undone_at` for an undone one.

`check:migrations` fails on a drop of either partial index, as for `article_versions_one_current` (`scripts/check-migrations/rules.ts`). The migration also inserts the `link_overviews` job row (disabled) and its $10 spending warning level; `seed-jobs.ts` upserts the same.

## How a story is linked

**Owed stories** (`services/overviewQueue.ts`): published, published within `overviews.backfillDays` (183) of now, with no active entry and no live settling `link` decision. `joined`, `founded`, `none` and `rejected` settle a story; `held` (its overview is frozen), `conflict` and `failed` leave it owed for the next run. A run takes the **backlog** (owed stories older than `nightHours`, 24) first, at most `backfillPerRun` (300), oldest first by publication date, and the **night's** owed stories (at most `perRun`, 60) only when that slice takes every older owed story. **While the backlog drains, new stories wait:** about 6 nights after the switch-on, the night's stories join the backlog and are linked after the older ones, so linking is strictly oldest first and no story is linked before an older one it may belong with (a night story founding with an October partner, say, before the August stories of the same development had their turn).

**Every window counts from the story's own date (`asOf` = its `datePublished`)**, in production as in the replay. For one story (`services/overviewLink.ts`, `linkStory`, pure over injected reads and calls):

1. **Candidates** (`services/overviewCandidates.ts`), in two parts so the replay runs the same code:
   - `nearestPublished`, SELECT only through the client given: published stories within `maxDistance` (0.45) by exact cosine distance (dedup's `MATERIALIZED` pattern, `.context/embeddings.md`), published on or before `asOf` and within `memberLookbackDays` (180) before it, nearest `neighbourLimit` (40); plus, whatever their distance, the earlier stories its research linked (its pool's `earlier` sources, else its newest completed legacy run's `earlier`) and its cluster's other published members.
   - `groupCandidates`, pure over a membership map (production's from `membershipsOf` as of the story's date, the sandbox's in memory alike): the overviews of neighbours that are active members, except one closed at its end event (a `faded` one stays, and reopens on a join), one whose newest entry on or before the story's date is older than the lookback (an entry published after the story never keeps an overview alive for it), and one an editor detached the story from; ranked by their nearest member, at most `candidateOverviews` (5). A frozen overview stays a candidate. **Founding partners:** neighbours with no active entry, within the distance and the 30 days before the story (`foundingWindowDays`), at most `foundingCandidates` (3).
   - No candidate: no call, and the story is settled `none`.
2. **Link confirmation** (`overview-link`, small tier, cached): the story (title, label, summary, day, up to 10 usable pool facts of pages as context), each candidate overview (title, charter, its 5 newest entries published on or before the story's date) as `O1`…, each founding partner as `F1`…. Per candidate: `belongs` (`yes`, `no`, `unsure`), the shared cause, place and end, each quoting both sides, the end-event flag, the stated confidence; for the story its role and kind.
3. **Acceptance** (`acceptLink`, code): a `yes` counts only with all three fields filled on both sides and each quote found in its side's text (`lib/quoteMatch.ts`, the answer keys' normalization): the story's title, label or summary; the overview's title, IN or END_EVENT, never its OUT list (which names what does not belong); the partner's title, label or summary. `unsure` is no. **The nearest passing overview wins** (a story joins one at most); if it is frozen the story is `held`. A founding partner is considered only when no overview passed. A refused yes with nothing else makes the story `rejected`; otherwise it is `none`.
4. **Founding** (two large-tier calls that must agree): `overview-found` drafts the charter (IN, OUT, an end event, which must be named) and the title from the two stories, the earlier as A; `overview-found-check` sees only the charter and each story and judges whether each fits and whether the end event is concrete. A draft with no end event, title or IN is `refused` without the check; disagreement founds nothing. Either way the attempt is a `found` decision and the story stays alone: `none`, or `rejected` when code refused a yes for one of its candidates; a call that gave nothing leaves it `failed`.

**Writes are ordered** (`linkInOrder`): link calls `concurrency` (3) at once, writes one story at a time in the run's order through `applyLink` (the switch is asked before each write and before any further call starts; once it is off, no call starts and nothing more is written, the calls in flight finish unwritten, and each story counts in the tally by its final result only), which in one transaction re-reads the memberships of the story and of the partners it was shown. A story that has an active entry or a settling decision by then (it was taken as a founding partner) is skipped. **A result whose partner has joined or founded an overview since is stale:** it is asked again once after the others, with fresh candidates, so two stories of one night proposing the same partner found one overview and the second joins it; stale again, it is stored as a `conflict`. A write the one-active-entry index refuses (an editor's attach at the same moment) is rolled back and logged as a `conflict`. Each outcome is the story's `link` decision; a founding also writes the overview (`forming`, open), both entries, its `found` decision and the partner's `link` decision. A join adds the entry, moves `last_entry_at`, and opens a quiet overview or reopens a faded one. **A join never changes a charter or a title.**

## Status, closing and reviews

**Three clocks** (`services/overviewStatus.ts`, pure). Quiet, closing and fading count in **development time**, `clock`: today, or while a backlog drains the newest story the run linked (`upkeepClock`), so a backfilled development is not judged stale by the months its backfill took. Reviews are spaced by the wall clock, `now`. The stands judge facts current on the real `today` (D-OV5): an overview speaks for now.

**The status machine** (`nextState`, applied by `applyStatus`, each move a `status` decision, applied only from the state it was computed on):

- **Unpublished members.** An active entry whose story is no longer published is detached (by the pipeline, not locked) and the story's link decisions are undone, so a story published again is linked afresh. This runs on frozen overviews too.
- **Scope** follows the active entries: one or none is `single`; a single overview grown back to two is `forming`. **Forming becomes ongoing (public) only through a review** keeping at least 3 entries (D-OV4), and an ongoing one cut below 3 stays public until its next review decides.
- **Status**, never on a frozen overview: open becomes `quiet` after `quietAfterDays` (21) without a new entry and opens again on one; after `fadeAfterDays` (120) it is closed as `faded`, and a join reopens it; an editor's closure never moves.
- **End-event closing.** A join flagged as reporting the overview's end event is judged by two large-tier calls that must agree (`services/overviewClose.ts`): `overview-close` sees the charter, the story and the overview's 5 newest other entries and must quote the words reporting the end event, found in the story's title, label or summary (`unquoted` otherwise); only then `overview-close-check` judges the end event against the story alone. Each check is a `close` decision on the story (`closing`, `not_happened`, `unquoted`, `disagreed`; `failed` is asked again next run). On agreement an open or quiet, unfrozen overview starts **closing** from the story's publication date. An entry newer than that story cancels the closing. After `closingDays` (7) the closure review runs; the next run closes it at its end event (`closed_at` in development time), unless the review detached the closing story, which cancels the closing.
- **Bursts** (`anomalies`): more than `burstLimit` (5) stories of one publication day joined by the pipeline within `nightHours` of now freeze the overview (a `frozen` status decision). Its burst review may clear the freeze; otherwise it stays frozen until an editor acts (phase 4). A frozen overview is linked to as `held` and gets no status move, no stands refresh and no review but the burst's.

## Reviews

`overview-review` (large tier, cache off, `services/overviewReview.ts`) re-reads an overview's active entries of published stories against its charter. **Due** (`reviewDue`), most pressing first: the burst's; the closure's, once the closing period is over; each time the active entries reach a count of `reviewAt` (3, 6, 12, 25, 50) that the last review kept fewer than; every `reviewEveryDays` (30) of wall-clock time while public and not closed. A single overview is never reviewed.

**A review only demotes.** Code turns its answer into the only actions allowed and refuses the rest, logged with the `review` decision: an entry judged not to belong is detached (`detached_by` review); one judged to belong with another role is relabeled; neither touches an entry an editor attached (`locked`). An id naming no shown entry (a story to add, a detached one to restore) or one judged twice is refused. **The charter is narrowed only by additions to OUT, which code appends to the current charter itself**: a rewritten IN or END_EVENT or a dropped OUT entry is refused, never applied. A changed title retitles. A freeze is cleared only when every entry is judged to belong. A review's detach keeps the story's link decision, so the story is not linked again. The scope then follows the entries kept (at least 3 ongoing, 2 forming, fewer single), never raised while the overview stays frozen; an overview made public for the first time gets its slug (its title's, with `-2`, `-3` … when taken), never changed after. A failed call acts on nothing and the next run asks again.

**The monthly audit** (`auditOverview`): every `auditEveryDays` (30), `auditSize` (5) random public overviews get the review's call from scratch at high effort; what it would have changed (wrong joins, wrong roles, the error rate) is logged as an `audited` review decision and **never applied**.

## Where this stands

`services/overviewStands.ts`, for every public overview (ongoing, not hidden) that is not frozen, whatever its status. **Overviews own no fact:** a line cites pool facts by row id.

1. **Facts offered** (`standsFacts`): the usable, structured page facts of the members' research-pass pools (`loadMemberFacts`: supported, not demoted, structured, never an earlier story's source), deduplicated by pool key, each judged current on today's date by story context's validity windows; current first, then newest, at most `maxStandsFacts` (60), with every `response` fact added, current or not. Each member's sources are numbered in turn, so refs are unique in the offer.
2. **When.** A refresh runs only when the **signature** (the facts offered with their current flags, the entries with their roles) differs from the last refresh that did not fail: an entry gained or lost, new usable facts, a fact demoted or expired, a role changed. With fewer than `minStandsFacts` (3) facts nothing is written and the current version is superseded (`too_few_facts`).
3. **The write** (`overview-stands`, medium tier, cached), from the facts alone, never from the previous version: at most `maxStandsSentences` (4) sentences and `maxDoingSentences` (2) lines of "What's being done", each citing offered facts.
4. **The checks, no repair round:** code drops a line citing nothing or anything not offered, a sentence stating a fact no longer current without that fact's date, and a line of what is being done citing a fact no longer current; then research's copying check and research's fact check (small tier) on every line, each built as a card's sentence is (`citedCheckItem`); a failed or unjudged line is dropped. A version is written when at least one sentence survives, superseding the current one (`written`); when none does, the current version stays (`none_kept`).
5. **Served** (`servedStands`, for phase 5's readers): a line citing a fact gone or no longer usable is dropped, as is one citing only unpublished members' facts, a sentence whose fact has expired since without its date, and a line of what is being done citing a fact no longer current.

## The job

`link_overviews` (`jobs/linkOverviews.ts`, "Link Overviews") is the last step of `daily_edition_chain`, after `publish_stories`; it is in `GOES_ON_PAST_FAILURE`, so a failed linking night leaves its own Error and failure notice, the chain run still succeeds and story context's after-publish part runs. It is in `MODEL_JOBS` (Flex) and `RESUMABLE_JOBS` (a story is settled by its decision, an upkeep item by its own; each written in one transaction).

**A run has five parts, in order:** (1) linking the owed stories; then the upkeep (`services/overviewUpkeep.ts`, `runUpkeep`, over the reads of `services/overviewUpkeepInput.ts`, SELECT only): (2) the end-event closing checks; (3) the status machine on every overview with an active entry, then the burst check; (4) the reviews due, read on the states as they stand after part 3, and the monthly audit; (5) the stands refreshes due. Each story and each overview is its own item: one that throws is logged and the rest go on. Before each write and each upkeep item it re-reads the switch and stops once it is off; what is left is owed to the next run.

Items for the failure share: checked = joins proposed (yes answers) plus stands lines the model's check was asked about; failed = yeses code refused plus stands lines dropped. The failed-stories notice fires when more than the failure share of at least `failedStoriesMin` (5) stories failed. Summary: "Linked 9 stories: 4 joined, 1 founded, 4 alone; 2 overviews refreshed, 1 reviewed", with held, left and failed counts after the linking, "; backfill 300 of 1,612" while a backlog is owed, and the closings begun, bursts, audits and failed upkeep items when there are any.

## How the switch stays off

Four layers, as for the research pass: **the eval gate** `OVERVIEW_EVAL_GATE` (`services/overviewGate.ts`, a code constant a test can mock and nothing at run time can change) refuses enabling (`ENABLE_CHECKS`) while set, and **while research passes' gate `PASS_EVAL_GATE` is closed**; **the order of switches**: enabling is refused while `research_pass` is disabled (`jobEnableConflict`); **the disabled row**; **the handler** does nothing unless both gates are open and the job is enabled ("Run now" on the disabled job does nothing). The owner opens the gate in its own change once the replay on the judged half meets the bars (plan phase 6).

## Evals

`eval:overviews --steps link,found,role` (`.context/model-eval.md`, "Overview evals"): one replay of a half's answer keys (entries and near misses, `developmentHalves.ts`, Ebola judged) in date order through production's `linkInOrder` against the in-memory sandbox (`scripts/eval/overviews/overviewSandbox.ts`, the lifecycle's rules), with production's neighbours read-only as of each story's date and kept to the replayed stories. Bars: joins right ≥97% and must entries found ≥85% (`link`); no wrong founding and the key's end event for every development, by the strong judge (`found`); roles agreeing ≥85% (`role`).

The upkeep's steps, the same pure code over the sandbox (`upkeepSandbox.ts`): `pass-pool` runs production's first pass in memory on the half's must entries, so the stands have the evidence passes would give; `stands` writes them at each development's checkpoints from its must entries and has the strong judge read every line kept (no unsupported sentence, latest key value stated ≥90%, "What's being done" present ≥70% where due and never unsupported; no verdict below 15 checkpoints with enough facts); `review` reviews each development with planted wrong joins, a flipped role and an emptied OUT (caught ≥80%, correct items demoted ≤3%); `closure` and `replay` replay linking and the upkeep day by day (no premature closure, ≥80% closed within 14 days of their end); `fact-check --facts stands` plants errors in the stands' lines. No paid run of these has been made.
