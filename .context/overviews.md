# Ongoing-story overviews (the `link_overviews` step, built and switched off)

An **overview** follows one ongoing development (an outbreak, a war, a negotiation, a rule-making) across our stories: a charter saying what belongs, a title, and a timeline of entries, one per story. The **link step** files each newly published story under the development it reports on, or starts a development when two stories report the same one. Plan: `.plans/2026-11-18_overviews-linking.md`; decision: ADR-0070. Re-centering: `.plans/2026-11-19_overview-recentering.md`, ADR-0071.

**What is built and what it does today.** Phases 1 to 5 of the plan: the records, the answer keys' version 2 and the halves; linking (candidates, the link confirmation, founding, roles); the upkeep after linking (end-event closing, the status machine, bursts, reviews and the monthly audit, "Where this stands"); the `link_overviews` job after publishing, the decisions log, and the evals of each part in an in-memory sandbox; the admin's pages, the story's Overview block, the editor's actions and Undo ("Admin", "Editor actions and undo"); what readers get once the step is on: the `/ongoing` pages, the story's `overview` field with its line and chip, the sitemap and link previews, and the reader's "Report an error" ("Public"); and phase 6 up to the switch: the methodology's section on ongoing stories, shown only while the step is on, and the runbook below ("Switching on"), so the switch is the gate's one line and the job's enable. Of the re-centering plan (`.plans/2026-11-19_overview-recentering.md`, ADR-0071), all of it is built: its records, charters that name no end event, the anchor and limit rules, an editor's re-center, the link's re-center signals, the automated re-center they fire (the upkeep's part 4) and its evals ("Re-centering"). **Nothing runs in production and readers see nothing:** the job row is seeded disabled, enabling it is refused while its eval gate is closed and while research passes' is, the public reads answer as if no overview existed while the step is off, and the admin's pages stay out of sight while the step is off and no overview exists. What is left is the owner's: the evals' paid runs and the switch.

## Writes

**One write model** (`services/overviewChanges.ts`, pure). Every write, the pipeline's (`services/overviewPlans.ts`: a join, a founding, a story left alone or in conflict, an end-event closing, the status machine's moves, a burst, a review, the monthly audit), an editor's and Undo's (`services/overviewEdits.ts`), is a plan of row changes made on the overview's rows as read, and one applier writes it (`applyPlan`, `overviewLifecycle/apply.ts`) in one transaction that locks the overview row (`FOR UPDATE`): the fields that follow the entries are settled (`settled`: the last entry, the newest active published entry's date; the scope as the status machine counts it), the slug given the first time the overview goes public, the decision written with its change set, and **each change applied only where its row still holds `before`**; one that finds otherwise throws and the whole write rolls back. **What an entry leaving does is one rule** every writer applies (`leaving`: a closing whose end-event story leaves is cancelled), whether the status machine, a review, an editor or an Undo moves it. A write that takes a public link away empties the caches holding links ("Public", `removesLink`). **The eval's sandbox applies the same plans to memory** (`scripts/eval/overviews/overviewSandbox.ts`, `upkeepSandbox.ts`, and the state they judge, `overviewStateOf`), and keeps the same change set on each decision (`changeSet`); a db test runs scripted nights through the job and through the sandbox and finds the same overviews, entries and decisions, each with the same change set, after every run (`jobs/linkOverviews.db.test.ts`, also the job's end-to-end test). It runs one link call at a time: with more in flight, which results go stale depends on how soon the write before them lands, sooner in memory than in the tables, so the two write the same stories by the same rules to the same end but in another order. **The cases the upkeep acts on are assembled once** (`services/overviewCases.ts`, pure): production's loaders fetch raw rows and the sandbox hands over its memory, and the same functions build the review case, the closing checks due and a re-center's parts from them.

**Charter limits: who may change what** (`services/overviewCharterRules.ts`, `amendCharter`, pure; ADR-0071's "who may widen what"). Every writer of a title or charter passes its rights to one rule: **a review** only adds OUT entries (a rewritten IN or END_EVENT, a dropped OUT entry or a locked-out one named again is refused); **an automated re-center** may also rewrite IN and END_EVENT, but never drops an OUT entry or names again one an Undo locked out; **a founding and an editor** write the whole charter, and an editor naming a locked-out entry again lifts its lock. All are held to the same limits, in one file both sides read (`shared/overviewCharterLimits.json`, into `config.overviews` through `lib/charterLimits.ts`; the admin's charter and retitle forms import it): a title of at most `maxTitleChars` (200) characters, an IN of `maxInChars` (1000), each OUT entry of `maxOutChars` (120) and at most `maxOutEntries` (12) in all (every link prompt copies the OUT list), an END_EVENT of `maxEndEventChars` (500). The prompts ask for an IN of one or two sentences and one END_EVENT; the answer keys' longest IN is under 600 characters and their longest END_EVENT under 350. A founding draft past any limit founds nothing and its check is not asked (`draftRefused`); a review applies the additions within them and refuses the rest; a re-center past one is refused before its check; an editor's is refused by the route (400). A review's and a re-center's judgments share one more rule: each ref's first judgment counts (`firstJudgments`).

## How a story is linked

**Owed stories** (`services/overviewQueue.ts`, the rule `services/overviewOwed.ts`, pure, which the replay runs too): published, published within `overviews.backfillDays` (183) of now, with no active entry and no live settling `link` decision. `joined`, `founded`, `none` and `rejected` settle a story; `held` (its overview is frozen), `conflict` and `failed` leave it owed, and **when it is linked again is bounded** (`owedStanding`, over its link decisions since its newest undone one, which owed it afresh):

- **Held:** while the overview that held it is still frozen, the story waits, with no model call and no new decision, however long the freeze lasts; once the freeze lifts it is due again at the next run.
- **Failed or conflict:** after each such attempt in a row (counted back to a held one, which the model answered), it is due again `retryAfterDays` (1, 3, 7) UTC days after the last; a failure or conflict on that last try **gives it up**: nothing links it again by itself (an editor's attach does, and an Undo or unpublishing that owes it afresh restarts the count). Each story given up is counted in the failed-stories notice ("The job"). A write that throws leaves no decision, so it is tried again the next run without counting.

A run takes the **backlog** (owed stories new to linking, older than `nightHours`, 24) first, at most `backfillPerRun` (300), oldest first by publication date; then the **retries** due, oldest first, in the room the backlog leaves of `backfillPerRun`; then the **night's** new stories (at most `perRun`, 60) only when the backlog's slice takes every older new story. Stories already tried never count toward that rule or take a new story's place, so however many wait, are retried or are given up, new stories are linked. **While the backlog drains, new stories wait:** about 6 nights after the switch-on, the night's stories join the backlog and are linked after the older ones, so new stories are linked strictly oldest first and none is linked before an older one it may belong with (a night story founding with an October partner, say, before the August stories of the same development had their turn).

**Every window counts from the story's own date (`asOf` = its `datePublished`)**, in production as in the replay. For one story (`services/overviewLink.ts`, `linkStory`, pure over injected reads and calls):

1. **Candidates** (`services/overviewCandidates.ts`), in two parts so the replay runs the same code:
   - `nearestPublished`, SELECT only through the client given: published stories within `maxDistance` (0.45) by exact cosine distance (dedup's `MATERIALIZED` pattern, `.context/embeddings.md`), published on or before `asOf` and within `memberLookbackDays` (180) before it, nearest `neighbourLimit` (40); plus, whatever their distance, the earlier stories its research linked (its pool's `earlier` sources, else its newest completed legacy run's `earlier`) and its cluster's other published members.
   - `groupCandidates`, pure over a membership map (production's from `membershipsOf` as of the story's date, the sandbox's in memory alike): the overviews of neighbours that are active members, except one closed at its end event (a `faded` one stays, and reopens on a join), one whose newest entry of a published story on or before the story's date is older than the lookback (an entry published after the story, or of a story unpublished before the job detached it, never keeps an overview alive for it), and one an editor detached the story from; ranked by their nearest member, at most `candidateOverviews` (5). A frozen overview stays a candidate. **Founding partners:** neighbours with no active entry, within the distance and the 30 days before the story (`foundingWindowDays`), at most `foundingCandidates` (3).
   - No candidate: no call, and the story is settled `none`.
2. **Link confirmation** (`overview-link`, small tier, cached): the story (title, label, summary, day, up to `linkFacts` (10) usable pool facts of pages as context), each candidate overview (title, charter, its `candidateEntries` (5) newest entries published on or before the story's date, the count an end-event check shows too) as `O1`…, each founding partner as `F1`…. Per candidate: `belongs` (`yes`, `no`, `unsure`), the shared cause, place and end, each quoting both sides, the end-event flag, for an overview its **scope** (`inside` its IN, `wider`: the same broader development outside its IN, or `unrelated`) and whether the story reports that the development became part of, or a response to, a larger one (`centerMoved`), each with the story's words as `signalQuote`, the stated confidence; for the story its role and kind. The scope and the center flag are the re-center's signals ("Re-centering"): they never decide a link.
3. **Acceptance** (`acceptLink`, code): a `yes` counts only with all three fields filled on both sides and each quote found in its side's text, **except for an overview whose charter names no end event: there the cause and the place decide**, its `end` is asked empty on both sides and not read (an end quoted anyway neither helps nor hurts); a founding partner and an overview that names one still need all three (`lib/quoteMatch.ts`, the answer keys' normalization): the story's title, label or summary; the overview's title, IN or END_EVENT (when it names one; an overview with none shows the model `END_EVENT: none (this development ends when reporting stops)`, never a quote source), never its OUT list (which names what does not belong); the partner's title, label or summary. `unsure` is no. **The nearest passing overview wins** (a story joins one at most); if it is frozen the story is `held`. A founding partner is considered only when no overview passed. A refused yes with nothing else makes the story `rejected`; otherwise it is `none`.
4. **Founding** (two large-tier calls that must agree): `overview-found` drafts the charter (IN, OUT, and an end event only when the development has one a report could date, such as a declaration, a signature or a ruling; most have none, left empty) and the title from the two stories, the earlier as A; `overview-found-check` sees only the charter and each story and judges whether each fits and, when one is named, whether the end event is concrete (`none` otherwise). **A charter needs no end event** (D-RC13, the owner's of 2026-11-19): an empty one is stored as null and the overview closes only by fading; a named one the check finds vague is a disagreement. A draft with no title or IN, or past a charter limit ("Charter limits": a title, IN, END_EVENT or OUT entry too long, too many OUT entries), is `refused` without the check; disagreement founds nothing. Either way the attempt is a `found` decision and the story stays alone: `none`, or `rejected` when code refused a yes for one of its candidates; a call that gave nothing leaves it `failed`.

**Writes are ordered** (`linkInOrder`): link calls `concurrency` (3) at once, writes one story at a time in the run's order through `applyLink` (the switch is asked before each write and before any further call starts; once it is off, no call starts and nothing more is written, the calls in flight finish unwritten, and each story counts in the tally by its final result only), which in one transaction re-reads the memberships of the story and of the partners it was shown. A story that has an active entry or a settling decision by then (it was taken as a founding partner) is skipped. **A result whose partner has joined or founded an overview since is stale:** it is asked again once after the others, with fresh candidates, so two stories of one night proposing the same partner found one overview and the second joins it; stale again, it is stored as a `conflict`. A write the one-active-entry index refuses (an editor's attach at the same moment) is rolled back and logged as a `conflict`. Each outcome is the story's `link` decision; a founding also writes the overview (`forming`, open), both entries, its `found` decision and the partner's `link` decision. A join adds the entry, joined at the run's time, and `last_entry_at` follows; a quiet overview opens again, and a faded one reopens, at the same run's status machine (part 3), which counts from that last entry. **A join never changes a charter or a title.**

## Status, closing and reviews

**Three clocks** (`services/overviewStatus.ts`, pure). Quiet, closing and fading count in **development time**, `clock`: today, or while a backlog drains the newest story the run linked (`upkeepClock`), so a backfilled development is not judged stale by the months its backfill took. Reviews are spaced by the wall clock, `now`. The stands judge facts current on the real `today` (D-OV5): an overview speaks for now.

**The status machine** (`nextState`, applied by `applyStatus`, each move a `status` decision, applied only from the state it was computed on):

- **Unpublished members.** An active entry whose story is no longer published is detached (by the pipeline, not locked) and the story's link decisions are undone, so a story published again is linked afresh. When that story began the overview's closing (`closing_story_id`), the closing is cancelled: the overview is open again, `closing_since` cleared, logged as an `open` status decision with reason `closing_cancelled` (the closure review reads only published members, so it could never detach that story itself). This runs on frozen overviews too.
- **Scope** follows the active entries: one or none is `single`; a single overview grown back to two is `forming`. **Forming becomes ongoing (public) only through a review** keeping at least 3 entries (D-OV4), and an ongoing one cut below 3 stays public until its next review decides.
- **Status**, never on a frozen overview: open becomes `quiet` after `quietAfterDays` (21) without a new entry and opens again on one; after `fadeAfterDays` (120) it is closed as `faded`, and a join reopens it; an editor's closure never moves. **A person's reopen counts as activity** (`reopened_at`: an editor's reopen, or an Undo of a closure): quiet and fading count from the later of the last entry and that reopen, so a reopened overview is not closed again at the next run.
- **End-event closing.** Only an overview whose charter names an end event closes this way; one with none closes only by fading (a join's flag on it is dropped when written, and `pendingClosings` passes it by). A join flagged as reporting the overview's end event is judged by two large-tier calls that must agree (`services/overviewClose.ts`): `overview-close` sees the charter, the story and the overview's 5 newest other entries and must quote the words reporting the end event, found in the story's title, label or summary (`unquoted` otherwise); only then `overview-close-check` judges the end event against the story alone. Each check is a `close` decision on the story (`closing`, `not_happened`, `unquoted`, `disagreed`; `failed` is asked again next run, as is `stale`, an agreement on an overview no longer open or quiet, or frozen, or whose charter's end event changed during the calls (a re-center), by its write: the calls judged the old end event, so the next run asks again under the one that stands). On agreement an open or quiet, unfrozen overview starts **closing** from the story's publication date, on that story (`closing_story_id`). An entry newer than that story cancels the closing. After `closingDays` (7) the closure review runs; the next run closes it at its end event (`closed_at` in development time), unless the review detached the closing story, which cancels the closing. A join whose closing an editor undid is not judged again.
- **Bursts** (`anomalies`, counted by `overviewStateOf`): more than `burstLimit` (5) stories published within the last `nightHours` (24) that the pipeline joined after `joins_counted_from` (the overview's last freeze, unfreeze, review or undo) freeze the overview (`frozen_by` burst, a `frozen` status decision). So a backfill's old stories never count, however many join in a night, and the joins one freeze answered never freeze it again at a run less than a day later once its review or an editor cleared it. Every such freeze raises a notice ("The job"). Its burst review, in the same run, may clear the freeze; otherwise it stays frozen until an editor unfreezes it or undoes the freeze. An editor's own freeze and the detach brake's ("Reviews") have no burst review and stay until an editor clears them. A frozen overview is linked to as `held` and gets no status move, no stands refresh and no review but the burst's.

## Reviews

`overview-review` (large tier, cache off, `services/overviewReview.ts`) re-reads an overview's active entries of published stories against its charter. **Due** (`reviewDue`), most pressing first: the burst's; the closure's, once the closing period is over; a reader's report that queued one (`report`, "Public"), until a review runs after it; an editor's re-center (`recenter`, "Re-centering"), likewise, which so reads every entry under the new charter (an Undo's lock queues none, and a frozen overview gets it once unfrozen); each time the active entries reach a count of `reviewAt` (3, 6, 12, 25, 50) that the last review kept fewer than; every `reviewEveryDays` (30) of wall-clock time while public and not closed. A single overview is never reviewed.

**A review only demotes.** Code turns its answer into the only actions allowed and refuses the rest, logged with the `review` decision: an entry judged not to belong is detached (`detached_by` review); one judged to belong with another role is relabeled; **neither touches a locked entry** (one an editor attached, detached and re-attached, set the role of, or restored with an Undo), and an OUT entry an editor's undo took out (`locked_out`) is never added again. An id naming no shown entry (a story to add, a detached one to restore) or one judged twice is refused. **The charter is narrowed only by additions to OUT, which code appends to the current charter itself**: a rewritten IN or END_EVENT (an end event of none copied back empty or as none is the same) or a dropped OUT entry is refused, never applied, and so is an addition longer than `maxOutChars` (120) characters or past `maxOutEntries` (12) entries in all, since every link prompt copies the OUT list. A changed title retitles, unless it is longer than `maxTitleChars` (200), the founding draft's limit too (the review's rights and these limits are one rule for every writer: "Charter limits"). **The detach brake** (`braked`): one review may detach at most half of the overview's unlocked entries. A review asking for more detaches none (each refused), clears no freeze and freezes the overview instead (`frozen_by` review) for an editor, and the job raises a notice; its relabels, narrowing and retitle still apply. So a review that misreads its charter never empties an overview in one night. A freeze is cleared only when every active entry is judged to belong, each by its own id (its first judgment counts): one left unjudged keeps the freeze. A review's detach keeps the story's link decision, so the story is not linked again. The scope then follows the entries kept (at least 3 ongoing, 2 forming, fewer single), never raised while the overview stays frozen; an overview made public for the first time gets its slug (its title's, with `-2`, `-3` … when taken), never changed after. A failed call acts on nothing and the next run asks again.

**A review applies only to the overview its call read** (`planReview`). Its write locks the overview and compares what the call was shown with the rows as they stand: the title, the charter, the scope, the freeze, and the active entries of published stories with their locks. When any of them changed during the call (an editor's action or Undo, a story unpublished), the review is recorded `stale` with what it asked and what it cost, and applies nothing, not even the brake's freeze (so no notice). It is not a failed item, and it stays due: the next run asks again on the overview as it stands. So a change made meanwhile is never overwritten (an editor's title, say), and an overview never goes public on entries that left during the call.

**The monthly audit** (`auditOverview`): every `auditEveryDays` (30), `auditSize` (5) random public overviews get the review's call from scratch at high effort; what it would have changed (wrong joins, wrong roles, the error rate) is logged as an `audited` review decision (`audit_failed` when the call gave nothing) and **never applied**. A failed audit counts as recent for `failedAuditHours` (24), so the audit is tried again the next day rather than at every run.

## Where this stands

`services/overviewStands.ts`, for every public overview (by the one rule: ongoing, not hidden, with its slug) that is not frozen, whatever its status. **Overviews own no fact:** a line cites pool facts by row id.

1. **Facts offered** (`standsFacts`): the usable, structured page facts of the members' research-pass pools (`loadMemberFacts`: supported, not demoted, structured, never an earlier story's source), deduplicated by pool key, each judged current on today's date by story context's validity windows; current first, then newest, at most `maxStandsFacts` (60), with every `response` fact added, current or not. Each member's sources are numbered in turn, so refs are unique in the offer.
2. **When.** A refresh runs only when the **signature** (the title, the facts offered with their current flags, the entries with their roles) differs from the last refresh that did not fail: an entry gained or lost, new usable facts, a fact demoted or expired, a role changed. **Before the pools are read**, `standsCases` compares a marker of what the offer is made of (the title, so a retitle or re-center writes the stands afresh; the members and their roles; each pool fact's id, verdict, status, structure, source and drop reason; computed in SQL) with the last settled refresh's, and skips the overview when it is the same and no offered fact has stopped being current since (`until`, the first day one does, `standsUntil`, kept with the refresh's `stands` decision). With fewer than `minStandsFacts` (3) facts nothing is written and the current version is superseded (`too_few_facts`).
3. **The write** (`overview-stands`, medium tier, cached), from the facts alone, never from the previous version: at most `maxStandsSentences` (4) sentences and `maxDoingSentences` (2) lines of "What's being done", each citing offered facts.
4. **The checks, no repair round:** code drops a line citing nothing or anything not offered, a sentence stating a fact no longer current without that fact's date, and a line of what is being done citing a fact no longer current; then research's copying check and research's fact check (small tier) on every line, each built as a card's sentence is (`citedCheckItem`); a failed or unjudged line is dropped. A version is written when at least one sentence survives, superseding the current one (`written`); when none does, the current version stays (`none_kept`).
5. **Served** (`servedStands`, for readers, "Public"): a line citing a fact gone or no longer usable is dropped, as is one citing only unpublished members' facts, a sentence whose fact has expired since without its date, and a line of what is being done citing a fact no longer current.

## The job

`link_overviews` (`jobs/linkOverviews.ts`, "Link Overviews") is the last step of `daily_edition_chain`, after `publish_stories`; it is in `GOES_ON_PAST_FAILURE`, so a failed linking night leaves its own Error and failure notice, the chain run still succeeds and story context's after-publish part runs. It is in `MODEL_JOBS` (Flex) and `RESUMABLE_JOBS` (a story is settled by its decision, an upkeep item by its own; each written in one transaction).

**A run has five parts, in order** (`services/overviewNight.ts`, `runNight`, pure, which the eval's replay runs over the sandbox too; the job adds the switch, the summary and the notices): (1) linking the owed stories; then, at the development clock (`upkeepClock`), the upkeep (`services/overviewUpkeep.ts`, `runUpkeep`, over the reads of `services/overviewUpkeepInput.ts`, SELECT only): (2) the end-event closing checks; (3) the status machine on every overview with an active entry, then the burst check; (4) the automated re-centers due ("Re-centering"), then the reviews due, read on the states as they stand after them (none for an overview re-centered this run: both calls judged every entry under its new charter), and the monthly audit; (5) the stands refreshes due. Each story and each overview is its own item: one that throws is logged and the rest go on. Before each write and each upkeep item it re-reads the switch and stops once it is off; what is left is owed to the next run. **The model calls are declared once** (`services/overviewSteps.ts`: each call's tier, its own effort where it has one, the monthly audit's high, its schema and its prompt), and production's `structuredCall` and the evals' cached calls both make them through `overviewCalls`; `runLinkOverviewsJob(now, calls)` takes them, so a test runs the whole job on scripted calls.

Items for the failure share: checked = joins proposed (yes answers) plus stands lines written within the limits; failed = yeses code refused plus those lines dropped by any check (code, copying, the model's). Each part's failed is at most its checked, so the share never exceeds 1. **Notices** (`.context/admin-notices.md`): the failed-stories notice when more than the failure share of at least `failedStoriesMin` (5) stories failed, and whenever a story was given up ("How a story is linked"), with how many in its title and text; the same rule over the upkeep's items (each end-event check, overview's status, re-center attempt, review, audit and stands refresh, each counted once whether its call gave nothing or its write threw, and the message names every kind; an attempt fails only when a call gave nothing), `upkeep-failing:link_overviews`; one per overview frozen in the run, by a burst or the detach brake (`overview-frozen:<id>`, linking its admin page); and one per automated re-center applied (`overview-recentered:<id>`, info: the old and new title and "Automatic re-centers: 1 of 2", or "2 of 2: only an editor can re-center it now"), since it changes what readers see with no person. Summary: "Linked 9 stories: 4 joined, 1 founded, 4 alone; 2 overviews refreshed, 1 reviewed", with held, left and failed counts after the linking, then the stories given up and those waiting on a frozen overview, "; backfill 300 of 1,612" while a backlog of new stories is owed, and the re-centers, closings begun, bursts, audits and failed upkeep items when there are any.

## Editor actions and undo

Odin confirmed the full set (plan, "Editor overrides and undo"). Each action and each undo is **one transaction of the lifecycle** (`editorAction`, `undoDecision`, `overviewLifecycle/editor.ts`), planned by pure code (`services/overviewEdits.ts`, `planEdit`, `planUndo`) as a change set and written as every write is ("Writes"), logged as the editor's decision. **A change applies only where its row still holds `before`**; otherwise nothing is written and the answer is "The overview changed meanwhile: reload the page and try again". An action that would change nothing is refused with its reason ("The overview is already frozen").

- **Attach** (`editor_attach`): a published story not in any overview ("The story is in "…": detach it there first"), never to a closed overview ("The overview is closed: reopen it first"); it reuses the story's detached entry here, or adds one (`joined_by` editor), `locked`, routine unless a role is given. **Detach** (`editor_detach`): `detached_by` the editor, reason `editor`, `locked`; when the story began the current closing, the closing is cancelled. **Role** (`editor_role`): `locked`, so a review never relabels it or detaches it. On the page an entry's role is picked in its select and written by its "Set role" button: a change of the select alone writes nothing (arrow keys move it option by option), and the select stays usable while the request runs. **A review never detaches or relabels a locked entry**, whoever locked it (an editor's attach, detach, role, or an Undo). The scope follows the entries as the status machine's does (one or none `single`, a single one grown to two `forming`); an editor never makes an overview public, only a review does.
- **Retitle** (`editor_retitle`): a later review may retitle again unless the overview is frozen. **Freeze or unfreeze** (`editor_freeze`; `frozen_by` editor), **hide or show** (`editor_hide`: hidden is never public).
- **Close** (`editor_close`, closed, reason `editor`): it never moves by itself and no story joins it. **Reopen** (`editor_close`, reopened): open, and counts as activity (`reopenedAt`).
- **Re-center** (`editor_recenter`, recentered): a new title and whole charter ("Re-centering").

**Undo** reverses one live decision by the change set it stored, the other way round (`inverted`, last first), and is itself an `undo` decision that names it (`target`); the reversed one gets `undone_at` and `undone_by`. **Whole or not at all:** it applies only while every row still holds what the decision left; when anything it changed was changed since, nothing is undone and the refusal names what ("This cannot be undone: its title changed after it. Undo the later change first."; for a review "…, and a review is undone whole or not at all"). Never put back: the fields that follow the entries (scope and last entry, settled again after the undo), a slug once given (it stays reserved), the burst count's start, and a review's own run (its time, the entries it kept, a closure's review). **Undoing an automated decision makes what it restores the editor's** (`stamped`): every entry it moves is `locked`, one it detaches detached by the editor with reason `undone`; a freeze it puts back is the editor's (`frozen_by` editor), which no burst review clears; an overview it takes out of `closed` is open again and counts as active from now (`reopened_at`).

- an editor's decision or an undo: its changes the other way round; **an undo of an undo** re-applies what was undone and makes that decision live again, and keeps the chain below it consistent at any depth (`undoChain`, over the overview's undos): **each undo's target is undone exactly while the undo is live.** So undoing the third undo of a join makes the first undo live again and the join undone again (its story owed again), and undoing the fourth makes the join live again; a decision undone again by the chain names the editor who undid the undo above it;
- a join (`link`, joined): the entry detached, reason `undone`, `locked`, and the link decision undone, so the story is owed again and may join another overview, never this one;
- a founding (`found`, founded): both founding entries detached the same way; with no other story joined since, that leaves the overview `single` with no entries and not public (stories joined since stay, and the scope follows them). The stories' link decisions stay live, so the pair does not found it again the next night. Undo on the link decision of a founding is refused: "Undo the founding instead";
- a closure (`status`, closed): open again, as a reopen, **only while the overview is still closed as that decision left it** (its day and reason), never after a later run closed it again; a closing begun (`close`, closing): open, the closing cleared, **only while the overview is closing on that story's report**, so undoing an old closing that an entry or a review cancelled never cancels a later one; a burst's freeze (`status`, frozen): cleared, **only while that freeze stands**, so it never lifts an editor's freeze or a later burst's. Only an Undo marks a decision undone, and a judged pair is never judged again either way (`pendingClosings`);
- a review (`reviewed`): the entries it detached back, its relabels, its OUT additions and its retitle restored, a freeze it cleared put back (as the editor's), all together. **What it restores becomes the editor's, so the next night's review cannot take it back:** the entries it brings back and the relabels it reverses are `locked`, and the OUT entries it takes out are locked out (`locked_out`; a review proposing one again has it refused, `allowedActions`). An ongoing overview's `entries_at_last_review` becomes the active entries the undo leaves, so the count trigger does not review it again at once; a forming one keeps its count, since only a review makes an overview public (D-OV4): it is reviewed when its count is due, and that review cannot detach the locked entries. An undo of that undo takes the locks off with the rest.
- a re-center, an editor's (`editor_recenter`) or automation's (`recenter`, recentered): see "Re-centering".

Nothing else is undoable (stands, audits, `none` or `rejected` links, unpublished detaches); one that recorded no change set, or whose changes are all of the kind never put back, is refused ("This decision did not record what it changed, so it cannot be undone", "Nothing this decision changed is left to undo"). The plan's "a role is undone by restoring the previous role" is the editor's role and a review's relabels: the pipeline writes no `role` decision.

## Re-centering

A development can move past the charter its first two stories gave it (a regional outbreak becoming a national one). **A charter may be widened or moved, by two paths only** (ADR-0071, reversing ADR-0070's D7 "never widen" by the owner's decision of 2026-11-19; plan `.plans/2026-11-19_overview-recentering.md`): an editor's re-center; and an automated re-center, two agreeing large-tier calls fired by what incoming stories say (the link call's `wider` and `centerMoved` flags), both built and, with the step, switched off. Both are change sets in the one write model, so Undo applies whole or not at all. Reviews still only narrow ("Reviews"). The rules both paths read are `services/overviewRecenter.ts` (pure), the constants `config.overviews`.

- **The anchor** (`anchorOf`, D-RC14): the entries a re-center must still cover, so an overview stays tied to where the development began. Its founding entries (the entries its `found` decision created) still active and published; with none left, the `anchorStandIns` (2) oldest active published entries stand in. Then every other active published turning point. A founding entry detached since, or whose story is no longer published, is no part of it: its removal already said it does not belong. **An automated re-center must cover every founding entry and at least `anchorTurningShare` (2/3) of the turning points, rounded up** (`anchorHolds`; 1 or 2 turning points must all pass), each judged to belong by both calls, so one misread in a long overview does not block it.
- **The limit:** `automated_recenters` counts applied automated re-centers not undone; at `maxAutomatedRecenters` (2) automation re-centers it no more and only an editor does (`automatedRecenterBlocked`: also only an ongoing overview, open or quiet and not frozen). An editor's re-center neither raises nor resets the count: it is the overview's lifetime count. Undo of an automated one puts it back.
- **The lock** (D-RC7): an editor's re-center in force (`recentered_by` `editor`), or an Undo of an automated one (`recenter_undone_at`), keeps automation from re-centering the overview for `editorRecenterLockDays` (180); the later of the two holds (`recenterLock`, which the admin shows with whose it is). The reviews' OUT additions and retitles carry on.

**An editor's re-center** (`editor_recenter`, `planEdit`; `PUT /api/admin/overviews/:id/charter`, the admin's "Edit charter"): a new title and the whole charter. It is the only way to drop an OUT entry or re-admit one an Undo locked out (naming it in OUT takes it out of `locked_out`), and it may name no end event; it works on a forming, quiet, closing or frozen overview and past the limit. The form lists the anchor under "This overview's anchor: a new charter should still cover these stories", and saving needs the box "The new charter still covers them, or I mean to move the anchor": the server records it (`inputs.anchorConfirmed`) and judges nothing, since an editor's action makes no model call. Refused: a closed overview ("The overview is closed: reopen it first"), no confirmation ("Confirm the anchor first"), the same title and charter ("The overview already has that title and charter"). It sets `recentered_at` and `recentered_by` editor, which is the lock and **queues a review** (`reviewDue`'s `recenter`, after `report`): demote-only and with the brake, it reads every entry under the new charter, so stories the editor's charter left behind are detached on review evidence, never silently, and locked entries stay. **A changed END_EVENT, to none too, cancels a closing** (D-RC10): the closing was begun on the old end event, which neither the closure review nor `pendingClosings` judges again; the overview is open, `closing_since`, `closing_story_id` and `closure_reviewed_at` cleared, logged as an `open` status decision with reason `recentered`, by `editor`. An IN or OUT change alone leaves a closing as it is. **It keeps `charter_model` and `charter_prompt_version`** (D-RC11), as a retitle does, so an editor-written title stays marked AI-written in the public marker (the cautious side; `.context/ai-transparency.md`, row 17).

**The signals** (D-RC3): the link call's `wider` and `centerMoved` flags for an overview it showed ("How a story is linked", step 2), each counted only with its `signalQuote` found in the story's title, label or summary (`signalsOf`), and only from a story active in no overview after its link (`countedSignals`: a story left alone, `none` or `rejected`, keeps every flag; a joined one only its `centerMoved` for the overview it joined; a held, conflicting, failed or founding story none, since a story that joined one overview and is wider for another is the merge question). They go into the `link` decision's inputs (`signals: [{ overviewId, kind: 'wider' | 'center_moved', quote }]`) with the run time it was linked at (`linkedAt`), under the partial GIN index `overview_decisions_link_signals`, found by containment (`signalRecords`, the key a literal in the SQL so the index serves it). **An attempt fires** when the live link decisions linked after the overview's last attempt (`recenter_tried_at`, the run's time as `linkedAt` is, so a run's own links never count twice) hold at least `recenterWiderSignal` (2) `wider` flags or one `centerMoved` (`signalsSince`, `signalFires`): the only guard against repeats, no calendar spacing. A night's check reads only the signals linked after the last attempt; the whole history is read only for an overview whose signals fire, since an earlier flag still offers its story. The admin shows the signals since the last re-center (since founding before one), at the limit too, and reads only those.

**An automated re-center** (`recenter` decisions; `services/overviewRecenter.ts`, `recenterOverview`; the reads `services/overviewRecenterInput.ts`, `recenterCases`, SELECT only): the job's part 4, before the reviews ("The job").
- **The case** (`recenterCaseOf`, pure, the sandbox's alike): an overview automation may re-center at the wall clock (`automatedRecenterBlocked`) whose signals fire; **the stories it may take in** (`turnedAway`, D-RC6): published within `recenterLookbackDays` (90) before the development clock and not after it, active in no overview, flagged for it by a live signal (one from before the last attempt still weighs as a story) or detached from it by a review; never one an editor locked there; newest first, at most `maxRecenterStories` (10). With fewer than `recenterMinJoins` (2) such stories no call is made and the signals wait for more. **The neighbors** (`neighbourOverviews`): the overviews `groupCandidates` gives at the development clock for its `recenterNeighbourEntries` (3) newest entries and those stories, itself excluded, each with its IN.
- **The draft** (`overview-recenter`, large tier, cache off) sees the title, the charter, the active published entries (title, label, summary, day, role, and `anchor="yes"` on the anchor's), and the stories with their signal quotes; it answers `keep`, or a new title, IN, END_EVENT (empty for none), the OUT list kept with any additions, judging every entry and story under it and giving each story a role. Its rules: re-center only when the stories show the development became part of or a response to something larger; the new IN must still cover every anchor entry (a center moves outward from where it began, never away). **Code refuses a draft before the check** (`draftRules`, rule `draft` or `out`, by the re-center's rights and the limits: "Charter limits"): no title or no IN, a title past `maxTitleChars`, an IN past `maxInChars` or an END_EVENT past `maxEndEventChars`, the same title and charter, a dropped OUT entry (D-RC2: only an editor drops one), an addition an Undo locked out, past `maxOutChars` or past `maxOutEntries`, or a judgment in the wrong list (a story's `S#` among the entries, an entry's `E#` among the stories: malformed, refused rather than failed, so it is not asked again every run); code builds the charter itself, the old OUT with the additions after it.
- **The blind check** (`overview-recenter-check`, large tier, cache off) sees only the proposed charter, the same entries and stories with no anchor marks, and each neighbor's IN; never the draft's reasons or the old IN. It judges each entry and story, whether a named END_EVENT is concrete (`none` for none), and whether the IN takes in a neighbor.
- **Code accepts** (`acceptRecenter`, D-RC4, D-RC14) only when every rule holds, and names each that fails: a named END_EVENT judged concrete (`end_event`; none is allowed); the anchor holds (`anchor`); at least `recenterKeepShare` (75%) of the entries judged to belong by both calls (`kept`; locked ones count, and are never detached); at least `recenterMinJoins` (2) stories judged to belong by both (`joins`); no neighbor covered (`neighbour`: merging is not a re-center's); and the detach brake on what it would detach (`brake`, checked on its own, since with many locked entries the share does not imply it). Only an unlocked entry outside the anchor that both calls reject is detached; one only one call rejects stays; only a story both accept joins.
- **Outcomes**, each one `recenter` decision with its rule and refusals, the anchor judgments, the signals that fired it, the stories offered and joined and the entries detached (`recenterInputs`): `recentered` writes the title and charter (`charter_model` the draft's model, `charter_prompt_version` the draft's, D-RC11), `recentered_by` `recenter`, `automated_recenters` + 1 and `recenter_tried_at`; detaches (`detached_by` and reason `recenter`; their link decisions stay live); and joins, a story with an unlocked detached row here reusing it, any other a new entry (`joined_by` `recenter`, no judgment record or confidence, the draft's model and prompt version), each active, unlocked and with the draft's role. **A re-center join never starts a closing** (D-RC12), `joins_counted_from` stays (the burst counts the pipeline's joins only), and **the slug never changes** (D-RC8). `kept` and `refused` only set `recenter_tried_at`. `failed` (a call gave nothing) and `stale` write nothing else and are asked again next run.
- **Stale** (`recenterStale`, `planRecenter`): its write locks the overview and compares what the calls read, as a review's does (title, charter, scope, freeze, the active published entries and their locks), and the status, `locked_out`, `automated_recenters` and whether every story it was offered is still published; for a proposal also each story to join, active in an overview by now or its row here no longer detached. A join the one-active-entry index still refuses rolls the write back and records the attempt `stale` on its own. An applied re-center raises a notice ("The job") and its overview's review waits for the next run.

**Undo of a re-center** inverts its change set whole, `recentered_at`, `recentered_by` and `automated_recenters` included (so an editor's lock lifts with it, and its queued review is no longer due), and is refused once anything it changed has changed since, the queued review's OUT addition or retitle among them ("This cannot be undone: its charter changed after it, and a re-center is undone whole or not at all. Undo the later change first."); a review that only detached entries does not block it. `recenter_tried_at` is one-way, so a later attempt never blocks an Undo. **Undo of an automated re-center** (`recenter`, recentered) also makes what it restores the editor's (`stamped`): its joins detached and its detaches restored, each `locked`; its OUT additions (`recenterInputs.outAdded`) locked out; the model fields back; the count down by 1, so an overview at 2 is under the limit again; and `recenter_undone_at` now, which is the lock but queues no review. `recentered_at` and `recentered_by` go back to the re-center before it, so **a chain undoes newest first**: after the Undo of a later automated re-center, the one before it (automated, or an editor's) can be undone too, and the Undo's lock stays. An undo of that undo raises the count again and lifts its lock.

## How the switch stays off

Four layers, as for the research pass: **the eval gate** `OVERVIEW_EVAL_GATE` (`services/overviewGate.ts`, a code constant a test can mock and nothing at run time can change) refuses enabling (`ENABLE_CHECKS`) while set, and **while research passes' gate `PASS_EVAL_GATE` is closed**; **the order of switches**: enabling is refused while `research_pass` is disabled (`jobEnableConflict`); **the disabled row**; **the handler** does nothing unless both gates are open and the job is enabled ("Run now" on the disabled job does nothing). The owner opens the gate in its own change once the evals on the judged half meet their bars ("Switching on").

## Evals

`eval:overviews --steps link,found,role` (`.context/model-eval.md`, "Overview evals"): one replay of a half's answer keys (entries and near misses, `developmentHalves.ts`, Ebola judged) in date order through production's `linkInOrder` against the in-memory sandbox (`scripts/eval/overviews/overviewSandbox.ts`, which writes by production's planners and write model, "Writes"), on production's calls as `overviewSteps.ts` declares them, with production's neighbours read-only as of each story's date and kept to the replayed stories. Bars: joins right ≥97% and must entries found ≥85% (`link`); no wrong founding, and no wrong end event where a founding names one, by the strong judge (`found`; a founding with no end event is never judged wrong, since a charter needs none, and the report says how many founded with none); roles agreeing ≥85% (`role`).

The upkeep's steps, the same pure code over the sandbox (`upkeepSandbox.ts`): `pass-pool` runs production's first pass in memory on the must entries the stands' checkpoints read (the newest 3 at each, `checkpointStories`: 38 of the judged half's 121), so the stands have the evidence passes would give; `stands` writes them at each development's checkpoints from its must entries and has the strong judge read every line kept (no unsupported sentence, latest key value stated ≥90%, "What's being done" present ≥70% where due and never unsupported; no verdict below 15 checkpoints with enough facts, and a rate on fewer than 20 judgments is the owner's to read); `review` reviews each development with planted wrong joins, a flipped role and an emptied OUT (caught ≥80%, correct items demoted ≤3%); `closure` and `replay` replay linking and the upkeep day by day (no premature closure, ≥80% closed within 14 days of their end); `fact-check --facts stands` plants errors in the stands' lines. The keys' responses and ends those bars read are drafted by `key-draft` and count only once the owner has marked them reviewed: `stands` refuses a half holding an unreviewed response, `closure` and `replay` one holding an unreviewed end. No paid run of these has been made.
