# Story context (context cards, the `build_story_context` step)

Story context gives a researched story **context cards**: how big the development is compared with what came before, why now, the build-up, who it reaches, what is being done, what comes next and a key term, each figure in the card of the question it answers (no In numbers card since 2026-11-17). A **box** is the stored record and a **card** is how the page shows it. The `build_story_context` step builds a story's boxes once, **before it is published**, so it goes out with them, from its research run's facts and from up to 3 searches of its own; every fact and every card is checked against its source, and what fails is dropped. A story published without them (while the step was off, by an editor, or late) gets them within 3 days from the step's second part, which the daily edition runs once it has published. An editor can gather a story's context again at any time ("On demand"), each run building on the earlier ones. **The step writes only its own tables** (`story_context_runs`, `context_facts`, `context_boxes`, `context_search_cache`): it never moves a status and never writes a story field or rating. **It never holds the edition**: in the daily edition publishing waits for no card (the step has just had its turn there, succeeded or failed), a failure of the step lets the chain go on to publish, and the published stories it still owes are built after publishing; only a publish run on its own waits for cards, at most two hours from a story's selection. **It is switched off**: seeded disabled, and enabling it needs the Tavily key and, by the plan, the eval gate (below). Overviews module 1 of `.plans/2026-11-16_story-overviews.md`; plans `.plans/2026-11-16_story-context.md` and `.plans/completed/2026-11-17_context-before-publish.md`. Decisions: ADR-0061 (records, reuse; its amendments of 2026-11-17: a second attempt in the same run, an editor's run on demand, and the step before publishing, which replaced "after publishing"; its note of 2026-11-17 on that amendment: no gate in the chain, the published stories after publishing, runs cut off by a restart), ADR-0062 (no person in the loop), ADR-0057's amendment of 2026-11-16 (numbers judged by the fact check), ADR-0063 (one named source, the tile rule; its amendment of 2026-11-17: no In numbers box, each figure in its slot's box, a card says nothing twice), ADR-0064 (no images; charts from fixed rules), ADR-0065 (always a constructive element; its amendment of 2026-11-17: no card when nothing sourced is found). Its successor, the research pass (`.context/research-passes.md`, ADR-0067), which writes a story's article and its cards in one step over one evidence pool, is built and wired beside it, switched off; it composes this step's stages (`structureFacts`, `gapStage`, `checkFacts`, `writeBoxes`) and changes nothing here until the owner switches it on, which switches this step off (enabling this step is refused while it is on). `eval:overviews --steps context` replays that pass now, not this step (`.context/model-eval.md`).

## The step

- **Third step of `daily_edition_chain`, in two parts** (`SPLIT_STEPS`, `jobs/handlers.ts`; `.context/scheduler.md`, "Chains"). **At its place**, between `research_stories` and `publish_stories`, it builds the stories about to be published, with their second attempts (`runBuildStoryContext('to-publish')`), so they go out with their cards (owner decision 2026-11-17, ADR-0061's amendment; from 2026-11-16 the step ran after publishing, and a story was live for a few minutes before its cards appeared). **Once the chain's last step has run**, a second run of the same job builds the published stories it still owes (`'published'`), while the step is on and such a story exists (`owesPublishedStories`), so publishing never waits for them (ADR-0061's last amendment of 2026-11-17; until then they came first in the step's one run, and the first night after switching the step on would have held the edition for 3 days of backfill). Each part is a run of the job: its own summary, usage, failure-share check and spend in the step's month. On a night with published stories owed, the Jobs page's last run of the step is the second part. A chain that pauses or stops before its end runs the second part once it reaches its end (after Continue, say); one cut off there resumes from the step. **Run on its own** ("Run now"), the job does both in one run (`'all'`): the stories about to be published with their second attempts first, then the published ones with theirs. **A failure of either part** (a database error reading its queue, say) **does not stop the chain** (`GOES_ON_PAST_FAILURE`): the step's Error and failure notice stand, and the chain goes on, to publish, the stories without cards. **No time limit:** both parts work through every story they owe, second attempts included, as research works through its per-run limit; the chain's deadline, after which the step started no other story, was dropped on 2026-11-17 with research's (owner, `20261117170000_chain_cutoffs_in_hours`; `.context/scheduler.md`, "Chains"). The first part is bounded by the night's researched stories about to be published. The cost: the edition publishes after the first part's run, about 5 to 15 minutes later on a normal night (Cost, below).
- **Which stories** (`nextForContext(queue)`, `services/storyContextQueue.ts`), every one the step owes, with no limit a run, `concurrency` (3) at once, in two queues: **the stories about to be published** (the first part), `selected` and unclustered or their cluster's primary (the stories `publish_stories` takes; a duplicate gets none), oldest selected first; **the published ones** of the last `lookbackDays` (3) (the second part), oldest published first: stories published while the step was off, published by an editor, or published without cards because the first part failed. Either way with a completed research run whose sources hold facts, and no context run yet. Single-source stories count once research ran on them; a story without a research run gets nothing. A selected story's day is the day of the run (`loadContextInput`): the day it is about to be published. **A story is settled by its first night's context run, whatever its outcome**: a failed, skipped or interrupted run is never tried again on a later night (a failure that may pass gets one more attempt within the same run, Failures below). These are the step's rules only: an editor can run it on any published story with research facts, whatever its age and however many runs it has had ("On demand", below). **The step takes a story's first run only**: its claim (`claimContextRun` with `firstRun`) refuses, under the run lock, a story that has any run, so a story an editor's run holds when the step reaches it, or that an editor gathered after the step read its queue, is skipped with nothing written: that run settles it.
- **The publish gate** (`awaitingContext`, `services/storyContextQueue.ts`, called by `publish_stories` beside research's `awaitingResearch`): **only for a publish run on its own** (Run now on the Jobs page), **while the step's job is enabled and `TAVILY_API_KEY` is set**, the publish leaves out a selected story the step owes (as above) with no settled context run (none, or one still `running` that no restart cut off; a completed, skipped or failed run settles it, and so does one a restart cut off, Interrupted runs below), **for at most `config.research.pipeline.maxWaitMinutes` (120) after it was selected**, research's bound (`.context/story-research.md`, "The pipeline step"). **In the daily edition it holds nothing** (the publish run is a step of the context step's own chain, `enclosingRunJob`, `lib/runUsage.ts`): the step has just had its turn there, succeeded, failed, or held elsewhere past the chain's wait for it, so a story whose cards failed or did not come in time goes out without them that night (ADR-0061's last amendment of 2026-11-17; until then the gate held such a story for the next publish run, which in the chain is a day later). The gate matters when a person runs `publish_stories` by hand while the step's run is building a story's cards or before it. A story it holds goes out at the next publish run, as research's do. While the step is off, nothing waits. An editor's publish never waits.
- **The switch is the job's flag** on the Jobs page; the job is seeded disabled (migration `20261116225456_story_context`, `scripts/seed-jobs.ts`). Enabling is refused while `TAVILY_API_KEY` is unset (`jobEnableChecks.ts`: "story context needs web search"). A run without the key builds nothing and says so ("Web search is off, no context built"). Switching the step off keeps the cards already built, as research's switch does; hiding them is the admin's (below).
- **"Run now"** does what a scheduled run does, the disabled job's included, so a trial run writes cards that readers see. The job's note says so.
- **Flex:** the step is in `MODEL_JOBS` (`jobs/modelJobs.ts`) with its own Flex switch; it usually runs before the chain's flex cut-off, 4.5 hours after the 23:00 UTC start (03:30).
- **Interrupted runs:** a `running` run a restart cut off (`staleBefore`, `services/storyContextStale.ts`: older than `staleRunMinutes` (120), **or started before this process did**, since a deploy or restart ended the process that ran it) is marked `failed`/`technical` ("interrupted: the server restarted during the run") at the next run's start, at every claim of a run row and when the admin reads a story's context (`markInterruptedContextRuns`), and settles its story; the publish gate counts it settled by the same rule. So after a deploy during the night's step, the resumed step finds those stories settled, and an editor can gather a cut-off story's context again at once instead of two hours later (2026-11-17). **Only the process that runs the schedule** judges by its start: a second process (`SCHEDULER_ENABLED` off, `.context/scheduler.md`) cannot tell the scheduling process's live runs from dead ones, so it waits out the 120 minutes. Left open: during a zero-downtime deploy the old process drains for seconds after the new one has started, and a run of its that would have finished in that window is marked anyway, its result dropped. The store is conditional on the run still being `running`, so a run marked meanwhile writes nothing. `build_story_context` is in `RESUMABLE_JOBS` (`jobs/interruptedRuns.ts`).
- **Failures:** a step's failure inside one story's run (a parse failure of a required call, a Tavily error) ends that run as `failed`/`technical` with what it spent and nothing else stored. What it spent includes every search that completed: a story's searches are all waited for, and when one fails after its retries, the others' credits still go into the run's `tavily_credits` and `cost_usd` and its record's `searches` (with nothing kept), since Tavily billed them. An error outside the run records a failed run for the story (`recordFailedRun`), and the next story goes on. The job never throws for a story.
- **A second attempt in the same run** (owner decision 2026-11-17, ADR-0061's amendment): a technical failure that may pass (`transientFailure`, `services/storyContextRuns.ts`: a rate limit, a server error, a timeout or a lost connection, once the SDK's and `withRetry`'s own retries are spent, on a required model call or a search) is tried once more after every other story of its queue, on its own run row (`runStoryContext(storyId, deps, { runId })`): the row goes back to `running`, its start the second attempt's (so the stale limit measures that attempt alone; until 2026-11-17 it kept the first's, and a long night's second attempt could be marked interrupted while it ran), and stores only the second attempt's outcome, record, facts and boxes, while its `cost_usd`, `model_usd` and `tavily_credits` add both attempts' (the store increments them; the job's usage counts both attempts' calls and searches anyway). A second failure settles the story like any other. A parse failure, a refused request, an answer of the wrong shape, an error of ours and an error outside the run are not tried again. The pipeline carries the flag (`ContextCallResult.transient`, `ContextDeps.transient`, `StoryContextResult.transient`); the eval sets neither, so its runs are never tried twice.
- **The failed-stories notice** (`jobs/buildStoryContext.ts`): when more than `jobs.failureShare.share` (20%) of the stories a run took failed technically, after their second attempts, from `failedStoriesMin` (5) stories on, the step records one warning notice, title "Build Story Context: 3 of 9 stories failed in the last run", linking to `/admin/jobs`, deduped as `stories-failing:build_story_context` (a repeat reopens it). Nothing pauses, and those stories get no cards.
- **The failure share** (`jobs/buildStoryContext.ts`, `jobs/failureShare.ts`): the run's item tally (below) goes into its usage (`recordItems`). When more than `jobs.failureShare.share` (20%) of at least `minItems` (20) checked items failed, the run's finish records one warning notice, as for any job that counts items (`.context/scheduler.md`, "Failure share"): title "Build Story Context: 14 of 45 checked items failed in the last run", this step's own message, linking to `/admin/jobs`, deduped as `step-failing:build_story_context` (a repeat on a later night reopens it), and the Jobs page shows the same line under the step from the last run's usage (`failureShareWarning`). **Nothing pauses**: the step runs the next night as usual (owner decision 2026-11-16).
- **The summary line:** "Built the context of 9 stories: 31 cards kept, 4 of 120 checked items failed, 1 story failed (2 tried twice)", always with the stories that failed, with the second attempts when there were any; or "No story about to be published owed its context". The second part's line names "published stories" ("Built the context of 2 published stories: …", or "No published story owed its context"), and a run on its own says "No story owed its context" when it had nothing. A publish run on its own adds how many stories wait for their cards ("Published 8 stories, 1 waiting for context cards").

## On demand

An editor's run of one story, the admin's **Gather context** ("Gather more context" once the story has a run; owner decision 2026-11-17, ADR-0061's amendment). `services/storyContextOnDemand.ts`; the button is `.context/admin-dashboard.md`'s.

- **Which stories:** any published story with a completed research run whose sources hold facts (`contextEligibility`): no lookback, any number of earlier runs, and the step switched on or off. Web search must be on, and research passes off: while they are on, a pass writes the cards with the article (`.context/research-passes.md`, "The admin"). `POST /api/admin/stories/:id/context/run` (Admin, below) answers 409 while `TAVILY_API_KEY` is unset or research passes are on (for any story), 404 for an unknown story, 409 for a story not published or without research facts, and 409 while a run of the story is running; nothing is written for a refusal.
- **One run of a story at a time**, the step's or an editor's: every run row is claimed (`claimContextRun`) and a failed one reopened for its second attempt (`reopenFailedRun`) under one transaction-level advisory lock, after marking stale runs, and either is refused while another run of the story is running (`services/storyContextRuns.ts`); the step's claim also refuses a story that has any run (its first run only, "Which stories" above). An editor's start then gets its 409; the step skips the story. A run a restart cut off is marked at that claim and no longer refuses it (Interrupted runs, above).
- **The run** is the step's (`executeContextRun`: the same pipeline, checks and store), as a one-story background task of type `context` the admin polls (`taskRegistry`; its story counts as processing, so a shutdown waits for it). A failure that may pass is tried once more on the same row right away, as the step does after its other stories (Failures, above); the task fails with the reason on a second failure, on a failure that would fail the same way, and for a run marked interrupted. A run cut off by a restart is marked failed as the step's are (Interrupted runs, above), and the admin's view marks it before it answers.
- **Building on earlier runs** (`loadEarlierWork`, `services/storyContextInput.ts`; `ContextInput.earlier`): what the story's earlier **completed** runs did. A failed, skipped or running run counts for nothing: it stored no facts, so what it searched or read is not taken as done.
  - Their supported, non-demoted facts are **carried over**, each once by its content (`factKey`: normalized URL, supporting sentence and statement), under new source ids after research's (numbered with the reused facts), each judged current or not on the story's day as research's facts are (Validity, below: an expired one only as a past value, never on a tile). They are not checked again, and are stored with the new run (`reused_from_id`) only when a kept box cites them. A fact a run copied is not carried itself: its original is.
  - A research fact an earlier run judged (supported, rejected or demoted alike, by its content) is **not structured or checked again**; with none left, there is no structure call.
  - The gap list judges the slots over the old facts and the new together, so a covered slot gets no search; **nothing missing: no search**, and the write call rewrites the cards.
  - A normalized query an earlier run made is **not searched again**, whatever its age (the 7-day cache aside), nor a `response` angle (`action`, `elsewhere`) an earlier run searched: the two-angle rule holds, each angle searched once over the story's runs. A skipped search takes no place among the 3, and is recorded (`skippedSearches`). A `response` whose angles were searched before counts as looked for, so `responseNotFound` still holds.
  - A page an earlier run read (a result it kept, a search fact's URL) is dropped as `read`; its domain stays open.
- **The cards:** the write call writes from the old facts and the new together, and the new run's boxes are the newest, so the page shows them. Nothing is deleted: the earlier runs' rows stay. **The editor's choices carry over by box type** (`editorChoices`, `services/storyContextRecords.ts`), from the newest earlier run that wrote boxes: a hidden card's type stays hidden (`demoted`), and a card an editor moved keeps its place (`place_by_editor`); a type new to the run starts as written.
- **Cost:** about a nightly run's, less when earlier searches and facts are reused; no cap, as for the step. The run goes in a run scope of `build_story_context` at standard price (a person waits on it, so no flex), and its spend is added to that step's `job_month_spend` row as one run, with that step's warning level checked (`.context/scheduler.md`, "Spending"); it is not editor research.
- **The eval** loads no earlier work, so it measures first runs only.

## What one story goes through

`runStoryContext(storyId)` (`services/storyContextRuns.ts`) writes the run row (`running`), loads the input (`loadContextInput`; none: the run is `skipped`, outcome `no-research`), runs the pipeline with production's deps and stores the result in one transaction. `buildStoryContext(input, deps)` (`services/storyContextPipeline.ts`) does no I/O of its own: its deps are the model call, the search and the opt-out policy, so the eval runs the same code. Production's call (`productionCall`) is one structured-output call on the step's tier after `rateLimitDelay()`, never in `withRetry` (the SDK retries), made by `structuredCall` (`services/structuredCall.ts`), which research's meter uses too. Tiers (`CONTEXT_STEP_TIERS`): every step small, the write medium.

| # | Step | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Load | `storyContextInput.ts` | The story (title, summary, its places: `storyContextLayout.ts`), the newest completed research run with facts (its sources `S0`… with facts, outlet, title, date, kind and voices), and the **reusable facts**: the supported, non-demoted context facts of the earlier stories research linked in `run.earlier` (an earlier story a research pass gave a pool offers that pool's facts instead, `.context/research-passes.md`, "Input"), kept only while current on this story's day, newest first, each fact once (an earlier story's stored copy of a fact it reused counts as that fact: the newest is offered), at most `maxReusedFacts` (30). Our own earlier stories' facts (`E…`) are never loaded: they are our text. A production run also loads what the story's own earlier runs did (`loadEarlierWork`, "On demand" above); a story's first run finds nothing there. |
| 2 | Structure (pass 1) | `buildStructurePrompt` | One call returns the structured fields of every research fact by id (value, value high, unit, approx, provisional, what is counted, place, as-of, credited body and kind, validity). Answers count only for the ids asked; a fact left out is not checked and counts as failed. A range whose two ends are equal is stored as a single value (here and in step 6), so a chart can draw it. |
| 3 | Gap list | `buildGapListPrompt`, `planSearches` | One call: per slot (`compared`, `why_now`, `build_up`, `who_affected`, `response`, `next`, `key_term`) whether a reader needs it and whether the current facts cover it (yes, partly, no), the story's own sentence reporting the response, if any, two `response` queries (`responseSearches`: `action`, who is responding and what is being done; `elsewhere`, what has worked against the same problem in other places or times) and up to 3 searches for the other slots. Code: `response` is always needed (ADR-0065) unless the story reports it (the quote must stand in our text, `supportFound`; else it is ignored). While the facts do not cover it (`covered` is not `yes`) and the story does not report it, **its two searches go first, one per angle** (owner decision 2026-11-17, ADR-0065's amendment); an angle the gap list left empty gets a query from the title (action: its first 9 words and "response"; elsewhere: its first 6 and "solutions that worked elsewhere"; either sets `responseFallback`). Then the other slots' searches, dropping those for slots not needed or covered; a repeated query runs once; at most `maxSearches` (3) in all, so an open `response` leaves one for the rest. None left: no search. |
| 4 | Search (pass 2) | `storyContextSearch.ts`, `storyContextSources.ts` | Tavily, basic depth, topic `general`, 5 results with raw text, ending on the story's day, starting 30 days before only when recent data is wanted. A query asked within `searchReuseDays` (7) is answered from `context_search_cache` at no credit cost (the key: normalized query, depth, topic and the recent flag, not the end date). |
| 5 | Filter | `dropOptedOut`, `pickNewSources` | Publishers' opt-outs (`researchSourcePolicy`, ADR-0056), checked before a result is cached and again on every answer; research's `BLOCKED_DOMAINS`; a URL or domain already among the story's sources (or a reused or carried fact's URL); a page an earlier run of the story read (`read`); a result without text; at most `perQuerySources` (2) a search and `maxNewSources` (5) in all. Drops are recorded with their reason. The text is chosen as research chooses it (`selectedText`). |
| 6 | Extract (pass 2) | `buildContextExtractionPrompt` | One call per kept source, in parallel: `usable`, the source's kind and outlet, and at most 5 facts answering the wanted items, each with a statement, the supporting sentence verbatim, the structured fields and its slot. Sources get ids after research's and the reused ones' (`S3`…), facts `S3F1`…. One failed or unusable extraction skips that source only. |
| 7 | Fact code checks | — | A pass-2 fact without a statement or sentence fails its structure (counted as left out). `supportFound` is recorded; the passage (the sentence with one either side, at most 600 characters) is kept. |
| 8 | Fact check | `factCheckItems`, research's `buildFactCheckPrompt` | Research's prompt and schema, unchanged, at most `checkBatchSize` (25) items a call. Each item is the fact as `renderFact` renders it ("Area burned in Indonesia: about 2.88 million hectares, as of 2026, according to KLHK (estimate)"), its evidence the sentence (pass 2: the passage) plus the source's date fact (`publishedFact`), its number hints as research gives them. A rejected or unjudged fact is stored with its verdict and never offered or shown. |
| 9 | Write | `buildBoxesPrompt` | One call (medium) writes at most 6 boxes from the supported facts and the reused ones, each with a slot, a heading (at most 8 words), a teaser (at most 8 words, the phone's one-line note; a short card, a single number or one short sentence, shows in full there instead and never uses it: `.context/public-website.md`, "Story context"), its place (`P<n>`, `W<n>` or `section`), 0–3 sentences citing fact ids and at most one visual (tiles, bars or line, with `apart` points for a different measure beside bars). **A figure goes in the box of the slot it answers** (a count of people in `who_affected`, a cost in `response`, a past value in `compared`), a box may hold its tiles alone, and a figure that answers no needed slot is left out; until 2026-11-17 the call also wrote an In numbers box (`figures`) of tiles, which gathered unrelated numbers in one card (owner: "the thing that supposedly holds it together is that it's numbers. But that's not useful"; ADR-0063's amendment). **Nothing on a card is said twice**: a tile's label says what the heading does not, empty for a box's one tile whose heading says it, and names a period only when it differs from the date of the box's newest fact, which the footer shows. |
| 10 | Box code checks | `structuralBoxes`, `resolveVisuals` | First the caps the prompt states, held in code (`maxBoxes` 6, `maxSentences` 3, `maxTiles` 4): the first box of each slot is kept and a second dropped, a box past the sixth, a sentence past the third and a tile past the fourth are dropped in the order written, each a structural failure. Then a cited id that is not a supported fact drops its sentence, tile or point (structural failure); a place the story lacks becomes the section; a tile with no current fact is dropped; a chart that breaks the chart rules is not drawn, and its box keeps its sentences (no failure). The copying check (`checkCopying`, a 12-word run against the sources' lines) runs on box sentences. |
| 11 | Box check | `boxCheckItems`, `applyBoxVerdicts` | Research's fact-check prompt again, with the sources' lines, so it judges copying as well as support: every sentence, tile ("people under smoke: about 12.5 million people"; an empty label reads as the box's heading) and chart ("area burned in Indonesia, hectares: 2015, 2.6 million; 2026, 2.88 million (estimate)") against its cited facts, numbers spelled as the facts render them (`spelledFigure`). A failing or unjudged item is dropped, and a box left with nothing in it. **No repair rounds** (ADR-0061). |
| 12 | Response not found | — | When pass 2 searched for `response`, no supported fact answers it, no kept box is a `response` box and the story does not report the response, the run records `responseNotFound` and **no card says so** (owner decision 2026-11-17, ADR-0065's amendment; until then a fixed-wording "honest gap" card did). |
| 13 | Store | `storeContextResult` | One transaction: the run's status, outcome (`built`, `none-kept`), box count, costs and record; every fact with its verdict, and the reused and carried facts a kept box cites, copied into the run with `reused_from_id`; the kept boxes in order, each with the newest as-of of its facts, the writer's model and prompt version, and the editor's choices on the earlier cards of its type. |

**The item tally** (`itemTally`): checked = facts supported, rejected, unjudged and left out, plus box items dropped as structural and box items judged; failed = facts rejected, unjudged and left out, plus box items dropped as structural or by the check. Charts not drawn under the rules and technical failures do not count; a run that failed counts nothing.

**Prompt versions** (`CONTEXT_PROMPT_VERSIONS`): the first 8 hex characters of a sha256 over the step's prompt built from a fixed canonical input, plus its JSON schema, computed at load. Each model-written fact and box stores its model id and its step's version.

**Untrusted input:** fetched text reaches only the extraction prompt, escaped and marked untrusted as research's is (`UNTRUSTED`, `tag` from `prompts/research.ts`); every parsed answer is XML-unescaped (`unescapeXmlDeep`).

## Validity

A fact's `validity` (`isCurrent`, `services/storyContextVisuals.ts`): `fixed` is always current; `slow` for `slowValidDays` (365) and `fast` for `fastValidDays` (14) from the start of its as-of period (`2026-11` counts from Nov 1), else from its source's date; without either it is not current. **Current is judged on the story's own day, never today**: a one-off story's cards describe the time of the story, and the footer's as-of date says so. Expiry decides which reused facts are offered, what the gap list may count as covering a slot, which facts a tile may show, and that the writer may state an expired fact only as a past value with its date.

## Tiles and charts

**The tile rule** (`tileDisplay`; ADR-0063): of a tile's facts, only shown (supported, not demoted), current ones with a figure count. The headline is the newest from the most authoritative kind present: official, research, news, other (the credited body's kind, else the source's: `official-body` is official, `news-report`, `analysis` and `opinion` news, `press-release` other). Every other such fact dated on or after it with a different value is shown beside it, each naming its source; a value the headline gives is not repeated. A tile with none is not shown.

**A tile's label says only what the card does not** (`tileLabel`, `services/storyContextLabels.ts`, applied when the page is served, so cards stored before 2026-11-17 get it too; ADR-0063's amendment): a trailing year that is the card's as-of year goes (", 2026", "in 2026", "as of 2026", "(2026)"), since the footer's "As of …" gives it; and on a card with one tile, a label whose content words (function words and plural "s" aside) all appear in the heading goes, so the number stands under the heading alone. A label with any word of its own stays whole. Which chip the page shows is the client's (`.context/public-website.md`, "Story context").

**The chart rules** (`validChart`; ADR-0064): every point a shown fact with a single value (a range is no point; such points are removed before counting), one unit across the points, no negative value (the axis starts at zero); bars need 2 points, a line 3 at different as-of dates, drawn in date order. An `apart` group (a different measure shown beside a comparison, never in it) is kept beside bars only, when it holds points of one unit. A provisional point is drawn hatched; a point within 30 days of the story's day is `recent` (the area's color). A chart not drawn leaves its box's sentences.

**Numbers come formatted** (`formatFigure`): "12.5m", "2.6bn", "400,000", "400,000+", "about 2.9m", "under 5,000", "5,000–5,400", a symbol unit kept ("$52m", "63%", "1.5°C"), with `estimate` for a provisional figure.

## Places

A story's places (`storyLayout`, `services/storyContextLayout.ts`): a researched story's paragraphs `P1`… and Why This Matters points `W1`… from `stories.research`, each with the URLs of the sources it cites; a single-source story's summary as `P1` and its relevance reasons split as the page splits them (`parsePoints`, mirroring the client's) as `W1`…, each citing the story's own article.

**Where a box sits is worked out when the page is served** (`placeBoxes`, `services/storyContextPlacement.ts`, pure), so a change of mode or limits reaches every story at once. One entry point decides it for the public view, the admin and the eval alike (`servedBoxes` and `placeServed`, `services/storyContextServed.ts`, pure): only the boxes the page serves compete (not hidden, something still shown; "What readers get"), each by the sources of every fact it cites, a chart's `apart` points included (`boxRefs`).

- An editor's place (`place_by_editor`) is kept in every mode, under the same limits; editor-placed boxes are placed first.
- Otherwise `STORY_CONTEXT_PLACEMENT` decides (`config.storyContext.placement`, default `model`): `model`, the place the write call chose; `random`, a draw among the paragraphs and points that cite one of the box's sources (by normalized URL), seeded by the story id and the box's index, so a page never reshuffles (a second-search box has no such spot and goes to the section); `section`, none beside the text.
- Then the limits, in box order: at most `notesInText` (2) beside paragraphs, `notesByPoints` (2) beside points, one per paragraph or point; the rest go to the section. Every box may sit beside the text (until 2026-11-17 the In numbers box always sat in the section). A place the story no longer has (it was edited) becomes the section.

## Records

- **`story_context_runs`**, one per run, the step's (one per story) or an editor's (any number; a second attempt reuses its row, its costs both attempts', its `started_at` the second attempt's). Which started a run is not recorded: `story_id` (SET NULL on delete, so its cost stays counted), `research_run_id` (the run it read, SET NULL), `status` (`running`, `completed`, `skipped`, `failed`), `outcome` (`built`, `none-kept`, `no-research`, `technical`), `boxes`, `cost_usd` (model plus credits at `research.tavilyUsdPerCredit`; model calls at standard price, so an upper bound), `model_usd`, `tavily_credits`, `result` (`ContextRunRecord`: the gap list's slots, `responseInStory`, `responseFallback`, the searches with cache hits, credits, result counts, kept URLs and a `response` search's `angle`, the searches not made again (`skippedSearches`), source and extraction drops, fact counts (`carried`: taken over from the story's earlier runs), each dropped fact with its problem, box drops by cause, number hints, `responseNotFound`, the item tally, every call with its tokens, cost and model, `optOutChecked`), `error` (sanitized: our own step messages and Tavily's, never fetched text), `started_at`, `finished_at`.
- **`context_facts`**, every fact a run structured or extracted, with its verdict: `ref` (unique per run), `origin` (`research`, `search`), `reused_from_id` (the fact a kept box's copy came from: an earlier story's, or this story's earlier run's); evidence `source_url`, `source_outlet`, `source_title`, `source_date`, `source_kind`, `supporting_sentence` (verbatim), `passage` (pass 2 only), `support_found`; the structured fields (`statement`, `value`, `value_high`, `unit`, `approx`, `provisional`, `counted`, `place`, `as_of` with `as_of_precision`, `credited`, `credited_kind`, `validity`); `verdict` (`supported`, `rejected`, `unjudged`) with `verdict_problem`; `status` (`unreviewed`, `reviewed`, `demoted`); `model`, `prompt_version`. **No page text is stored**, only the sentence and the short passage.
- **`context_boxes`**: `position`, `type` (the seven slots; the enum keeps `figures`, the In numbers box written until 2026-11-17, for its stored rows, which are never read back: `contextRecords` reads only the slots' boxes, `SLOT_TYPES`, so such a box is not served, not listed in the admin and not editable, and a run that wrote only one does not count as the newest run with boxes; no migration deletes them), `heading`, `teaser`, `sentences` (`[{ text, factIds }]`), `visual` (a stored tiles, bars or line spec citing refs, `storedVisualSchema`), `as_of` with its precision, `place` (`P<n>`, `W<n>`, or null for the section), `place_by_editor`, `status` (`demoted` is hidden), `model`, `prompt_version`, `written_at`. The `gap` column is unused since 2026-11-17 (always false; it marked the honest gap, and dropping it would be a destructive migration). Boxes cite their own run's facts by ref; facts and boxes cascade with their story and run.
- **`context_search_cache`**: `key`, `query`, `results` (URL, title, date and chosen text of each result that passed the opt-out check), `credits`, `created_at`. Rows past `searchReuseDays` are deleted at each run's start (`pruneSearchCache`): a short-lived processing copy.

## Reuse

Three kinds (ADR-0061 and its amendment of 2026-11-17): the exact-query cache above; the earlier linked stories' current, supported facts (step 1), offered under new source ids and stored with the run only when a kept box cites them; and the story's own earlier runs ("On demand"). The run records cache hits (`searches[].cached`), reused facts (`facts.reused`), carried facts (`facts.carried`), the searches not made again (`skippedSearches`) and the pages already read (`read` drops). If the cache's hit rate stays near zero for a month, the cache goes (follow-up). A cached answer is reused for a story up to 7 days apart, so its results may end a few days after an older story's day.

## Cost

No budget stops the step (owner decision 2026-11-16, ADR-0060). A story's calls are fixed in number: at most 3 searches (1 credit each at basic depth), 5 extractions and about 7 model calls, about $0.03 a story, at most about $0.10 with no cache hit, twice that for a story tried a second time (Failures, above). A run takes every story it owes (no limit since 2026-11-17, owner's request): a day's selection, about 4 to 11 stories, costs at most about $1.10, more only by the second attempts; the first night after the step is switched on also fills the published stories of the 3 days before, about four times that. **Time** (an estimate: no night has run the step yet): a story's run takes about one to three minutes (about seven model calls in sequence, its searches and extractions in parallel), 3 at once, so a normal night's first part adds about 5 to 15 minutes before publishing. The published stories still owed, the first night's 3 days of them included (up to about an hour), are the second part's, after publishing, and delay no edition. No time limit bounds the first part since the chain's deadline went (2026-11-17, owner; The step, above): the night's stories bound it, as research's per-run limit bounds research, so a slow night delays publishing by the first part's whole length. One case is longer: a person's "Run now" of the step still running when the chain reaches it is waited for, up to the chain's `stepWaitMinutes` (120), before the chain goes on to publish (`.context/scheduler.md`, "Chains"). The step's spend is in the Spending overview (`.context/scheduler.md`, "Spending"), with a warning level of $20 seeded with the job. A run's model cost and credits are kept apart on its row; `job_month_spend` gets them through the run's usage.

## What readers get

`GET /api/stories/:slug/context` (`routes/public/stories.ts`, `services/storyContextView.ts`), `Cache-Control: public, max-age=60`. **Not in the OpenAPI contract**, like `/appearances`, so modules 2 and 3 can still change the shape. Only a published story's boxes of its newest run that wrote any, not demoted, in order, unless a research pass wrote cards: then the newest such pass's, and no context run's (`cardRecords`, `services/storyCards.ts`; `.context/research-passes.md`, "The cards a pass writes"; no pass runs while that step is off); a sentence citing a fact no longer shown (rejected or demoted) is dropped with it, and a box left with no sentence and no visual is left out before places are worked out (so is an honest gap stored before 2026-11-17; an In numbers box stored before then is never read, Records above). A story without context, unknown or not published gets `boxes: []`, so cards built before publishing appear with the story when it is published, never before. The route reads the story's text and `research` (for its places) only once a box is served, so a story without cards costs two small queries.

```ts
{
  boxes: {
    id: string
    type: 'compared' | 'why_now' | 'build_up' | 'who_affected' | 'response' | 'next' | 'key_term'
    heading: string
    teaser: string             // the phone's one-line note (unused for a short card, shown in full)
    place: string              // 'P<n>' | 'W<n>' | 'section'
    sentences: { text: string; citations: { sourceId: string; sentence: string | null }[] }[]   // research's CitedText
    visual:
      | { kind: 'tiles'; tiles: { label: string /* may be '' (tileLabel) */; values: { text: string; estimate: boolean; sourceId: string }[] }[] }
      | { kind: 'bars' | 'line'; caption: string; unit: string
          points: { label: string; value: number; text: string; provisional: boolean; recent: boolean; asOf: string | null; sourceId: string }[]
          apart: { caption: string; unit: string; points: /* as points */ [] } | null }
      | null
    asOf: string | null        // 'YYYY-MM-DD' | 'YYYY-MM' | 'YYYY': the newest date its facts describe
    sourceIds: string[]        // the box's sources, first-cited order, for its footer
  }[]
  sources: {                   // research's ResearchedSource shape; ids C1… in first-cited order, one per normalized URL
    id: string; origin: 'original' | 'source'; outlet: string; host: string | null; url: string | null
    title: string; date: string | null; country: null; kind: string; storyId: null; slug: null
  }[]
  asOf: string | null          // the newest asOf of the boxes
  aiGenerated: { fields: ('heading' | 'teaser' | 'sentences' | 'visual')[]; model: string; digitalSourceType: string }
}
```

`aiGenerated.fields` is empty without boxes; `model` is the model recorded with the boxes (`contextAiMarker`, `lib/aiProvenance.ts`). The page carries `data-ai-generated="context"` on each note and on the section, and no visible label of its own: the story's "Researched and written by AI" line covers the cards (owner decision 2026-11-16), and on a story whose research run was never applied its "Summary and analysis by AI." line does (owner decision 2026-11-17; `.context/ai-transparency.md`). How the page lays the cards out is the client's (`.context/public-website.md`).

## Admin

Routes in `routes/admin/storyContext.ts` (mounted under `/api/admin/stories`, admin auth), services `services/storyContextAdmin.ts` and `services/storyContextOnDemand.ts`. 404 for an unknown story or a box of another story.

- `GET /api/admin/stories/:id/context` (after marking runs cut off by a restart failed) →
  ```ts
  {
    run: { id; status; outcome; startedAt; finishedAt; costUsd; modelUsd; searchUsd; tavilyCredits; boxes; error; result /* ContextRunRecord */ } | null,  // the newest run
    boxes: { id; type; heading; teaser; status; hidden; place /* where the page shows it now; its stored place, or section, when the page does not serve it */; storedPlace /* P<n>, W<n> or null */; placeByEditor; sources: { outlet; url }[]; asOf; position }[],  // the boxes the page serves (the newest research pass's, else the newest run's that wrote boxes), hidden ones included; placed as the page places them, on the story's day (today for a story not published)
    pass: { id; number; writtenAt } | null,  // the research pass that wrote the boxes, null for a context run's; the admin then shows it in place of the run
    places: { place /* P<n>, W<n>, section */; label /* 'Paragraph 1', 'Why This Matters 1', 'More context' */; text /* its start, 80 characters */ }[],
    placement: 'model' | 'random' | 'section',
    gather: { eligible /* published, with research facts */; refusal /* why Gather context is closed now: web search off, not eligible, a run running; or null */ }
  }
  ```
- `POST /api/admin/stories/:id/context/run` (rate-limited as an expensive operation) → 202 `{ taskId, runId }`: starts an editor's run ("On demand") as a one-story task, polled at `GET /api/admin/stories/tasks/:taskId`. 409 with the reason while it cannot start; 404 for an unknown story.
- `PUT /api/admin/stories/:id/context { hidden: boolean }` → 204: hides every box the page serves (`demoted`) or shows the hidden ones again (`unreviewed`): the newest pass's when a pass wrote cards, an older pass's and the context runs' left as they are. Nothing is deleted.
- `PATCH /api/admin/stories/:id/context/boxes/:boxId { hidden?: boolean, place?: 'P<n>' | 'W<n>' | 'section' }` (at least one) → 204: hides or shows one box, a context run's or a pass's, and moves it, setting `place_by_editor`. 400 for a place the story does not have; 404 for an In numbers box stored before 2026-11-17 (until then, moving it out of the section was a 400).

Hiding and moving are the one way a person changes what the step built (ADR-0062); an editor's run writes new cards by the step's own rules and checks, keeping those choices. Who hid a card, and when, is not recorded.

## Before switching on

The step is gated on `eval:overviews` (roadmap D8, ADR-0066): planted errors on context facts, structured fields, the gap list and searches, what's being done (ADR-0065), boxes, placement (below its bar the step goes live with `STORY_CONTEXT_PLACEMENT=section`) and cost, as means over 3 runs on the judged half of the gap keys of 30 one-off stories (`.context/model-eval.md`, "Overview evals": the `gap-draft`, `gap-keys` and `context` steps and `--facts context`). The keys are drafted and checked after merge; until they are committed, the `context` step has nothing to run on. The owner switches the step on. A change to a prompt, schema or model tier of the step reruns the gate before it ships. **The write's prompt and schema changed on 2026-11-17** (no In numbers box, a figure in its slot's box, labels that say only what the heading does not; ADR-0063's amendment), so the write's prompt version changed: any `context` step result from before that date is stale, and the gate's `context` step runs again on the new prompt before the step is switched on. **Since 2026-11-17 the `context` step replays the research pass's first pass** (its cards judged, its cost bar the whole pass's $0.20), not this step: its results gate the pass's switch (`.context/research-passes.md`, "Evals and the gate"), and this step has no replay of its own left.

**The methodology paragraph waits for the switch.** While the step is off, the methodology page says nothing about context cards, since "Many stories carry context cards" would be untrue on the live site. The owner-approved paragraph (2026-11-16; plan, "For Odin" 2) goes back into the "Researched Stories" section of `client/src/pages/MethodologyPage.tsx`, after the paragraph ending "…starting with the article where we first found the story." (a comment marks the spot), **in the same change that commits the eval's gap keys**, which lands right before the owner switches the step on. Word for word (on the page, "don't" is written `don&apos;t`):

> Many stories carry context cards: how big the development is compared with what came before, why it is happening now, who it reaches, what is being done and what comes next. An AI model reads the sources our research found and, where something a reader needs is missing, searches for up to three more. Every number on a card names its source, and when sources disagree we show both. A separate AI check compares each fact with the sentence it came from and drops what that sentence does not say. Each card gives the date its facts are from; we don't update cards later.

That change also sets `.context/ai-transparency.md` (the story-context row) and `.context/public-website.md` ("Story context") back to saying the paragraph is on the page.

## Modifying

- **A prompt** (`prompts/storyContext.ts`): read `.context/prompting.md`; the version changes by itself. Research's fact-check prompt is shared: a change there changes both checks here, and needs research's eval too.
- **A visual kind or rule:** `schemas/storyContext.ts` (`boxesSchema`, the stored schemas), `storyContextVisuals.ts`, the box items in `storyContextChecks.ts`, the view and the client.
- **The route's shape:** the client reads it; it is not a public contract, but a cached page may hold the old shape for 60 seconds.
- **A new place kind:** `storyContextLayout.ts` (and the client's `parsePoints` mirror), the admin's place regex (`contextBoxUpdateSchema`).
- **How points split:** change both `parsePoints` (server `storyContextLayout.ts`, client `lib/parse-points.ts`) and the case table both packages' tests run, `shared/fixtures/parse-points.json`.
