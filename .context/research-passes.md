# Research passes (the `research_pass` step, wired and switched off)

A **research pass** is one repeatable step over one **evidence pool** per story: it searches, extracts and checks facts into the story's pool, writes the article from the pool's supported facts (research's write, checks, repair and gate) and the context cards from the pool (context's write and box check). A story's first pass writes version 1 of its article; a later pass judges the current version against the facts found since and rewrites only the sections they make wrong or substantially different, with an Update note that says what changed and why. It is to replace the research step (`research_stories`, `.context/story-research.md`) and story context (`build_story_context`, `.context/story-context.md`) with one switch, one budget and one spending line. Plan: `.plans/2026-11-17_research-passes.md` (approved 2026-11-17; built in phases). Decisions: ADR-0067 (the step and its pool), ADR-0068 (versions, the later pass and Updates).

**What is built and what it does today.** The records (phase 1), the first pass and its input (phase 2), the later pass, article versions with undo and revert, and the `update` eval (phase 3), the evals' adapters, and the wiring (phase 4): the pass's lifecycle, the job in the daily edition, the publish gate, one switch for the old steps and the new one, the cap shared with research, the admin routes and the public `updates` field; and the interfaces (phase 5): the cards served from the newest pass, the admin's pass view, "Research again" and "Undo this update", and the Updates on the story page. **It is switched off, and nothing in production runs a pass** ("How the switch stays off"): the tables stay empty, the daily edition skips the step, the publish gate holds nothing for it, every editor's start is refused, `updates` is an empty list on every story, and so every screen shows what it showed before: the admin's Research tab its research runs and the old buttons, the story page no Updates, and the cards the context runs'. The version marks on "Revert to single-source" and a re-assess touch no row while no version exists. The old steps, their tables and their readers are unchanged until the retirement. Still to come: the eval report and the switch (phase 6), and the old steps' retirement (phase 7).

## The pool

A story's pool (`services/researchPool.ts`, the in-memory types; `services/passRecords.ts`, the rows):

- **Sources** (`evidence_sources`): the original article (`S0`), cluster members, confirmed neighbours and search results (`S1`, `S2`, …), and our own earlier stories research links (`E1`, …; origin `earlier`, keyed `story:<id>`, no URL). A source is one per page by its normalized URL (`sourceKey`), with its outlet, title, date, kind, country, relation, voices (with their stance), and whether the publisher's opt-outs were checked. A page a pass read and left out (an unusable extraction) is a source row with a `drop_reason` and no ref: it is never read again.
- **Facts** (`pool_facts`), refs `S2F3` (the source's ref and a number within it), stable within the story; one per story by `poolFactKey` (context's `factKey` over the source key, the supporting sentence and the statement), so a fact found again is not added again. Each fact holds its **evidence** (supporting sentence, a search fact's passage, whether code found the sentence in the text, the statement, what it measures, the slot it was searched for), its **structured fields** (value, range, unit, approx, provisional, counted, place, as-of with its precision, credited body, validity, with the structure call's model and prompt version; null until structured), its **extraction** (model, prompt version, the pass that found it) and its **judgment**: the verdict (`unjudged`, `supported`, `rejected`), the problem, the check's model, prompt version and pass. **A fact is checked once**: a pass checks only `unjudged` facts of pages, never one an earlier pass or a legacy context run judged, and never our own earlier stories' (our text). **Demotion is a status**, never a deletion: `status` `unreviewed`, `reviewed` or `demoted`, with `drop_reason` (`contradicted`, `editor`), the pass that demoted it and `contradicted_by`. A fact is usable when supported and not demoted (`usable`).
- **Searches** (`evidence_searches`): every query a pass or a legacy run made, coverage (research's, with its window and depth) or gap (context's, with its slot and `response` angle), its results and credits, and the pass (null for an imported one). A gap query made before is not searched again, nor a `response` angle; a coverage query only when an earlier coverage search made it over every day of the pass's window, so a later pass searches the first pass's queries again over the days since (an imported search, with no window recorded, covered none; `researchPassPlan.ts`).

## Records

- **`research_passes`**: one row per pass, numbered per story, with its trigger, kind (`first`, `later`), status (`running`, `completed`, `skipped`, `failed`), outcome, research's gate verdict on a first pass, costs (`cost_usd`, `model_usd`, `tavily_credits`), `record` (research's run record, context's run record, what became of the cards, the coverage queries skipped as made before, the coverage plan the pass used and why each step of it was reused or asked, `plan`), a sanitized error and the version it made. `story_id` is SET NULL on delete, as for research runs.
- **`article_versions`**: a story's article as each pass left it: number (one more than the story's highest, so an undone version's number is never reused), kind (`first`, `update`, `legacy`), status (`current`, `superseded`, `reverted`), the text columns an apply writes, the reader projection (`research`), the article with every fact id each section cites (`draft`, added by `20261118120000_article_version_draft`, nullable; null on a legacy version, whose facts are read from its citations, `versionArticle`), the changes (section, text before, text after or null when removed) and the Update note. **At most one `current` version per story**, by the partial unique index `article_versions_one_current` (raw SQL in the migration; Prisma cannot model it, and `check:migrations` fails on a migration that drops it, `.context/database-migrations.md`).
- **`pass_boxes`**: the cards a pass wrote, `context_boxes`' columns with the pass. Each takes over the editor's choices (hidden, moved) on the card of its type shown before: the newest earlier pass's cards, else the story's legacy context cards.
- **Writing a pass** (`storePassResult`, in the caller's transaction): fenced on the pass still `running` (nothing is written once a restart marked it cut off), then the pass row, its new searches, the pages it left out, its new sources and facts, the judgments it changed on stored facts, its version (the current one superseded) and its cards. **Reading** (`loadPool`, `currentVersion`, `storyVersions`, `newestPassBoxes`) is SELECT only, through the client given. The public reads take only what they serve: a story page's Updates read `publicVersions` (the versions not reverted, without drafts or story columns), and its cards `loadCitedFacts` (the pool facts the cards cite and their sources), never the whole pool.
- **The old tables stay read-only history**: `story_research_runs`, `story_context_runs`, `context_facts` and `context_boxes` are never written by a pass.

## Legacy import

`importLegacyPool(tx, storyId)` (`services/legacyPool.ts`) turns a story's legacy runs into pool rows and version 1, once, inside the transaction that claims the story's first pass (`claimPass`). The claim reads them first, before it takes research's reservation lock (`legacyPoolPresent`, `readLegacyImport`), so the lock every claim and research start waits on is not held through those reads; under the lock the import checks again that the story has no pool and writes what was read. From the newest completed research run whose sources hold facts (the one context reads): its sources and earlier stories, its facts `unjudged` unless a context run judged them (matched by `factKey`, taking that verdict and the structured fields, prompt version `legacy`), its coverage queries. From the context runs: their own search facts with their verdicts, as new sources, their gap searches, and the pages they left out. A story with `stories.research` set gets version 1 (`legacy`) from its columns and projection, so its next pass is a later pass judged against what readers see. It returns `imported`, `present` (the story has pool rows or versions: nothing happens) or `none`. Its reads are `readLegacyImport(db, storyId)`, SELECT only and writing nothing: the `update` eval builds its fixtures from them.

## Input

`loadPassInput(db, storyId, passId, now)` (`services/researchPassInput.ts`, SELECT only through the client given): the story as research's loader gives it (`researchStoriesByIds`: original, cluster members, neighbours, earlier-coverage candidates; null when research cannot run on it), the pool, the current version, the story as readers see it (title, summary and places from the current version, else from the story's columns), the story's day (the publishing day, else `now`'s), today, the day the story's last completed pass or legacy research run started (`lastPassDay`, never the running pass's; where a later pass's coverage window opens), the later passes whose facts the article never took a judgment on (`carried`: every later pass that ended after the story's last pass whose judgment the article took, which is a first pass that made a version, a later pass whose update was applied, or one that found nothing to change; so a failed pass, a budget stop, an update not applied and an `update_failed` are carried until a pass settles them), the coverage plan of the story's last pass that kept one, completed or failed, never the running pass's (`plan`, read from `record->'plan'` by `storedPlan`, a step whose entry does not parse kept as none; null for none), and the reusable facts of the earlier-coverage candidates (context's `reusableFacts`: supported, not demoted, current on that day, each once). **An earlier story with a pool offers its pool's facts** (supported, not demoted, structured, of its pages, never of our own text), never its context facts, which the legacy import took into the pool and a pass may have demoted there; one without a pool offers its context facts, so reuse goes on across the switch. They come uncapped; the pass offers only those of the stories it links, at most `storyContext.maxReusedFacts` (30). `loadFirstPassInput` is the same with an empty pool and no version, reading no pass table: the eval's replay of a first pass.

## The steps

`runResearchPass(input, deps)` (`services/researchPass.ts`) does no I/O of its own, like `researchStory` and `buildStoryContext`: the metered call, research's coverage search, context's gap search, the opt-out policy and `transient` are injected, so the eval runs the same code. It never throws. A first pass:

1. **Own sources, coverage queries, search and extraction**: research's (`extractCoverage`), with the pool's pages left out of the candidates (`knownPages`, drop kind `known`) and the queries an earlier pass already searched over every day of this pass's window skipped (`coverageSearches`, recorded as `skippedQueries`). Research's sources and facts join the pool, each source once by its page and each fact once by its key; research's extraction prompt is not versioned, so its facts carry `research-extract`. **The pass's extraction asks for each fact's structured fields too** (since 2026-11-18, a research cost change: `coverageExtractionSchema`, the structure step's rules in the extraction prompt), and a fact that comes with them is stored structured, by the extraction's model and `research-extract`; research's own run keeps the plain extraction. Each step's answer goes into the pass's coverage plan (`record.plan`, "The later pass").
2. **Structure** (context's call) on the pages' facts that have no structured fields: only those whose extraction gave none (an answer without them, a fact the legacy import or an earlier pass stored unstructured).
3. **Gap list and searches** (context's `gapStage`) over the pool and the offered earlier facts, the searches planned by `planSearches` (`response` first from both angles while not covered and not reported, then the other slots), at most `research.pass.maxGapSearches` (3), skipping what any earlier pass searched; pages the pool holds or left out are not read. The gap facts join the pool.
4. **Fact check**: research's fact-check prompt through context's `checkFacts`, once on every `unjudged` fact of a page.
5. **Article**: research's write, checks, repair rounds, derive and gate (`runPassArticle`) from the pages' **supported** facts and our earlier stories' facts, every source shown to the copying checks. A kept draft becomes version 1 (`first`).
6. **Cards**: context's write and box check (`writeBoxes`) from the pages' supported, structured facts and the offered facts of the earlier stories the pass links, on version 1's places when it made one, else the story's own. An offered fact a kept card cites joins the pool (`reused_from_id`), and the card cites it by its pool ref. An earlier story's fact the pool already holds (a pass joined it, or a page says it) is never offered again: the gap list and the cards see it once, under its pool ref, and not at all once the pool demoted it, so every ref a card cites is a fact of the pool.

Rules:

- **Cards never fail a pass.** A technical failure of steps 2-3 or 6 completes the pass with its article and records `cards: failed`; a budget skip in step 6 records `cards: budget`. A first-pass fallback (no article) still writes cards for a story with research facts; **a story research found no fact in gets no cards**, as context's rule had it.
- **A budget skip before the cards ends the pass** as research's `fallback: budget` does (outcome `budget`), with no article and no cards. A technical failure of the fact check fails the pass (`status: failed`): the article needs its verdicts.
- **Outcomes:** `written` (a kept draft), `stale`, `contradicted`, `research-rating`, `fallback`, `budget`, `technical`.
- **The extraction steps run at `config.research.extractionEffort`** (`RESEARCH_EXTRACTION_EFFORT`, default `none`; `EXTRACTION_STEPS`: `extract`, `gap-extract`, `structure`; `passStepEffort`) on their tier's model, research's own runs included; every other step keeps its tier's effort (`.context/llm-analysis.md`).
- **Step names** (`PASS_STEPS`, `PASS_STEP_TIERS`): research's steps, the card steps, named apart from research's steps of the same name (`structure`, `gap-list`, `gap-extract`, `facts-check`, `cards-write`, `box-check`, each on its context step's tier, `CARD_STEPS`), and the later pass's `judge`, `update` and `note` (medium; the `update` eval decides whether one moves). `passCalls` lists every call of a pass under these names, `cardFacts` the pool's facts as context reads them, `cardsLayout` the places the cards were written for.

## The later pass

A story with a current version gets a later pass (ADR-0068): steps 1 to 4 as above, its coverage window from `lastPassDay` (else the story's day) to today, with the earlier-coverage candidates published since the window opened and not linked yet (`laterWindow`, `laterStory`); a coverage stop is recorded and the pass goes on with what it found.

**The coverage plan is reused while nothing invalidates it** (since 2026-11-18, a research cost change; `planSteps`, `researchPassPlan.ts`). The neighbour confirmation and the queries are the plan's two steps; each answer is kept under its step's key, a hash of the prompt's text and its schema (`planKey`), so a change to the story's title or summary, its neighbours, the article, the prompt or the schema changes the key. A later pass takes the stored answer to a step whose key is the one it would ask under and makes no call (the confirmed neighbours by their candidate numbers; the queries before the cut to `queryCount`, searched again over the new window as before); it asks a step whose key changed, or that the last pass kept no answer to. A first pass asks both. No answer is kept for a call that gave none (a failed confirmation, no queries written), so the next pass asks again. `record.plan` holds the answers used and, per step reached, why: `reused`, `first pass`, `no stored plan`, `changed` or `no neighbours`. The model is not part of the key: a stored answer outlives a tier's model change until the prompt changes. Then the article step (`updateArticle`, `services/researchPassLater.ts`, its rules pure in `researchPassUpdate.ts`), on the facts found since the article last took a judgment (`fresh`): those this pass added to the pool and those of the passes it carries (`carried`, "Input"), so the facts of a pass that failed are judged by the next one:

1. **Judge** (`buildJudgePrompt`, `judgeSchema`), only when the pass found a usable fact of a page: each section of the current version (with the facts it cites) against the fresh facts, `unchanged`, `wrong` or `substantially_different`, naming the facts; and the old facts the fresh ones contradict.
2. **Demotions** (`demotions`): an old usable fact a fresh fact contradicts is demoted, its pass, reason and contradicting facts recorded, and the pool takes it whatever the outcome. **A newer value is not a contradiction**: when every contradicting fact measures the same thing (normalized) as of a later moment (structured `asOf`, compared at the precision both give), the old fact is `superseded` and stays.
3. **Targets** (`rewriteTargets`): a section citing a demoted fact (this pass's or an earlier one's) is always one (`demoted-fact`, flagged by code); one the judge flags is one only when the facts it names include a fresh or demoted fact (`rewrite`), else it is `unfounded` and stays; when the opening paragraph is one, the title, summary and blurb are taken up to be checked against it (`follows-opening`). No target: `unchanged`.
4. **Rewrite** (`buildUpdateRewritePrompt`: research's repair prompt framed as an update, from the usable facts only) of the targets alone, merged by `withRepairs`, then research's checks and repair rounds on what it changed. A section still failing is dropped when the article can do without it, recorded as a change with no text after; when it cannot (`UNDROPPABLE`), the outcome is `update_failed` and **the article stays as it is** (no automatic unpublish; the apply raises one admin notice for the story, "Applying a pass").
5. **The new version**: every section no change names keeps the very text and citations readers saw (`withFrozen`), the quote its source. Each source id the version before lists keeps that version's source; where the rebuilt projection gives such an id to another source (a legacy version's numbering is not the pool's), the rewritten sections cite it under a new id. The changes (`versionChanges`) list each rewritten section under its new label and each dropped one under its old, the title, summary and blurb when their text changed, and each carries the label it had in the version before (`from`), the one label no two changes share (a section rewritten after a dropped one moves up into its label). Readers' Updates, the fixed line and the eval name a change by `from`, as its earlier text is; a change stored before `from` reads its own label.
6. **The Update note** (`buildNotePrompt`, `noteSchema`): from the changes and the facts they rest on (those the changed sections cite now, and the demoted ones they cited). Code checks it (`checkNote`: not empty, at most 60 words, no quotation marks, at most one em dash, citing known facts and at least one fresh or demoted fact), then research's fact check and both copying checks as a paragraph's, with one repair attempt. A note that still fails, or that the budget or a failed call leaves unwritten, is the fixed line (`fallbackNote`: "Updated: Story paragraph 2 rewritten and Caveat 1 removed after new reporting from Reuters."), citing the fresh or demoted facts. The record counts the notes the checks ran on, those a copying check flagged and those the fact check found unsupported.

Outcomes: `updated` (a new version, kind `update`), `unchanged`, `update_failed`, `technical`, and `budget`, which ends the pass. **A technical failure fails the pass** (`status: failed`): a judge or rewrite call that failed, or a check round that threw, ends it with that call's `transient`, so it is tried once more in the run where the failure may pass, and the step counts it among its failed stories (the failure share and the failed-stories notice); it writes no cards (`cards: kept`), its demotions stay in the pool, and its facts are carried to the next pass. A note call that fails is no technical failure: the note is the fixed line (step 6). **Cards are written only when the pool changed**: a later pass that gained no supported fact of a page and demoted nothing keeps the cards (`cards: kept`); otherwise step 6 runs on the new version's places. Cards never carry an Update.

## Versions and Updates

`services/articleVersions.ts` (pure): a version's article (`versionArticle`), its sections as readers see them (`sectionTexts`), the projection with untouched sections frozen (`withFrozen`), the next version (`nextVersion`), a first pass's Update on a published story (`firstPassUpdate`), the Updates readers get with the sources each cites (`publicUpdates`, `updatesForReaders`; "The public updates field"), and what an undo restores (`undoPlan`, `versionValues`). The writes are `services/articleVersionWrites.ts`, in the caller's transaction:

- **Undo this update** (`undoUpdate`, `services/researchApply.ts`; `POST /api/admin/research-passes/stories/:storyId/undo`): only the newest version, and only an update (refused on a first or legacy version, or when no earlier version is left). The previous version's fields go back on the story through research's fenced write, and in the same transaction the current version becomes `reverted` and the previous one `current`, each fenced on its status (a 409 when the story changed meanwhile), and **the facts the undone update's pass demoted are back in use** (`unreviewed`, their drop cleared): left demoted, the next pass would rewrite a restored section citing one with nothing new found. Another pass's demotions stay. Undoing twice walks back one update at a time.
- **Revert to single-source** (`revertResearch`) and a **re-assess** (`assessStory` on a researched story) mark every version `reverted` in the transaction that writes the story (`recordAnalysis`'s `also`), so no version stays current over text readers no longer see.

## Budget

One meter for the whole pass: research's `createRunMeter` (`services/researchCalls.ts`), which meters the card steps' and the later pass's calls (their desk output estimates in `STEP_OUTPUT_TOKENS`: judge 1,500, update 3,000, note 400) and the gap searches (at basic depth's credits; a cached answer costs nothing) as it meters research's, against the pass's ceiling and what the month's cap leaves. A call without room is `skipped`, a search without room throws `BudgetExhaustedError`. The ceilings are `config.research.pass.firstPassUsd` (0.40) and `laterPassUsd` (0.25), about twice a pass's target, reserved when the pass is claimed.

- **One monthly cap for research runs and passes** (`config.research.monthlyCapUsd`, ADR-0067): `monthToDateUsd` (`services/researchSpend.ts`) sums the cost of every research run and pass started this UTC month, each once, deleted stories' and running reservations included; a meter leaves out its own run or pass. Research's runs reserve against the same sum under the same advisory lock (`RESEARCH_RESERVATION_LOCK`), so a run and a pass started at once never pass the cap together. While no pass runs, the sum is research's alone, as it was.
- **Spending**: the step's month is `research_pass`'s `job_month_spend` row. A step run adds its usage when it finishes (`finishJobRun`); an editor's pass runs in a run scope of the step's job, adds its own usage in its task (`addMonthSpend`) and checks that line's warning level ($20, seeded by the migration). So the Spending page shows the step as one line in the daily edition's group, and `editor_research` and `build_story_context` keep only their past months.

## Running a pass

`services/researchPassRuns.ts`:

- **The claim** (`claimPass`), one transaction under research's reservation lock: the passes a restart cut off are marked first; refused while a pass of the story runs (one at a time); the story's legacy runs are imported once (`importLegacyPool`); the kind is decided (`later` when the story has a current version, the import's included, else `first`); refused when the month's spend plus that kind's ceiling would pass the cap; then the pass row is written `running` at its ceiling, numbered one more than the story's highest. Concurrent claims (the step's, an editor's, a research run's) never pass the cap or run one story twice.
- **Execution** (`executePass`): the input (`loadPassInput`; a story the loader cannot research ends the pass `skipped` at $0), the pass over production's deps (`productionPassDeps`: the metered calls and searches, the gap search with its 7-day cache and opt-out check, the opt-out policy, `transientFailure`), **a second attempt in the same pass** when it failed on a problem that may pass, then the apply. It never throws: an error outside the pipeline fails the pass at its reservation.
- **Cost**: a finished pass's `cost_usd` is the larger of what the meter counted (both attempts) and its model cost plus its credits at their price; a running pass counts at its ceiling, and so does one a restart cut off.
- **Interrupted passes** (`markInterruptedPasses`): a `running` pass past its stale rule (`passStaleBefore`, `researchPassQueue.ts`, over the restart rule context's runs share, `lib/runCutOff.ts`: older than `research.pass.staleRunMinutes`, 480, twice a research run's and a context run's limits since a pass makes both kinds of call and may try twice under one row, `RESEARCH_PASS_STALE_RUN_MINUTES`; or, in the scheduling process, started before it) is marked `failed`, `technical`, "interrupted: the server restarted during the pass", keeping its cost. It runs before every claim, every step run and every admin read. A cut-off pass settles its story.
- **Skipped passes** (`recordSkippedPass`): a pipeline pass that never started (the cap reached, a story the loader cannot research, a failure outside the pass) is recorded `skipped` at $0 with its reason, so it settles its story and counts nothing against the cap.
- **An editor's pass** (`startEditorPass`, `startEditorPassBatch`): checked (the switch, the status, the analysis research needs, a running pass, the cap) and claimed before the answer, then run in the background as a `research` task, at standard price (a person waits on it), in a run scope of the step's job. A bulk pass is one task over up to `research.bulkMaxStories` (50) stories, `research.pass.concurrency` (4) at a time, each claimed when its turn comes, so a later one sees the cost of those before it; a refused story is skipped with the reason. A pass the story never took (the story moved or was written during it, the step was switched off, or a restart cut it off) is counted skipped too, as "Not applied: …" with why, never as researched again.

## Applying a pass

`services/researchPassApply.ts`. **A pass applies itself** (plan, For Odin 3), the nightly pass and an editor's alike:

- One transaction writes the pass's rows (`storePassResult`) and, when it made a version, the story's columns and projection through research's fenced write (`write`, `researchApply.ts`), so neither lands without the other. **Every story write is fenced on the story as read before the pass**: an editor's move or edit during the pass wins, and the pass's rows are stored without its version, with the error "not applied: …". A pass a restart marked cut off before its store is dropped, and the story write rolls back with it.
- A pipeline pass's first `stale` or `contradicted` verdict rejects the story through `transition` (actor `pipeline`), which takes only a selected story. **An editor's pass never rejects a story** (owner, 2026-11-18): its verdict is recorded on the pass (`outcome`, `verdict`) and shown in the pass view as recorded only, and the story stays as it is for the editor to act on, as research's editor runs left it. Every other kept draft, `research-rating` included, becomes version 1, as research's step applies one; the single-source snapshot (`pre_research`) is taken once, as research's apply takes it. A later pass stores no gate verdict.
- **A first pass on a published story is an Update** (For Odin 5, `firstPassUpdate`): its note, which code words and dates ("Researched with 3 more sources on Nov 19; the story was rewritten from them."), and the single-source text section by section under the page's labels (the title, the summary as the story's one paragraph, the bullets), so readers can read what the story said before.
- **Updates start with publication** (owner, 2026-11-18; `versionForReaders`): a later pass's rewrite of a story not yet published keeps its changes, which the admin shows, but no note, so readers never get it as an Update or an "Updated" mark, also once the story is published.
- **A later pass's `update_failed` keeps the article** and raises one admin notice for the story (`research-update-failed:<id>`, reopened by the next such pass): nothing is unpublished or rejected.
- A pass that ends after the step was switched off stores its rows and applies nothing.
- After an apply, the outlets' favicons are fetched in the background.

## The job and the gate

`research_pass` (`jobs/researchPass.ts`, "Research Passes" on the Jobs page), seeded **disabled** by `20261117230407_research_passes` and `seed-jobs.ts`. It is a step of `daily_edition_chain`, after `build_story_context` and before `publish_stories`; a disabled step is skipped, so the edition runs the old steps until the switch. **A failure of the job as a whole stops the chain before publishing**, as research's does; a story's failure never fails it. It is in `MODEL_JOBS` (a Flex switch) and `RESUMABLE_JOBS` (it takes only stories with no pipeline pass, and a cut-off pass settles its story).

- **A run** marks cut-off passes, then **does nothing unless the step works** (the eval gate open, research's switch on: the key set, every model priced) **and its job is enabled**: a "Run now" of the disabled job reports why and researches nothing, unlike research's. It takes up to `research.pass.perRun` (20, `RESEARCH_PASS_PER_RUN`) owed stories, oldest crawled first, `concurrency` (4, `RESEARCH_PASS_CONCURRENCY`) at once, with no time limit. Before each story it re-reads that state and starts no other once the step is switched off; a pass in flight finishes and is stored without being applied. A story whose turn failed outside its pass gets a $0 skipped pass. Its summary: "Researched 12 stories: 9 applied, 1 rejected, 1 failed, 1 not applied, 3 left for the next run".
- **Failure share** (`.context/scheduler.md`): the items are the cards' checked items plus the article's sections, each once however many rounds checked it (`passItemTally`: every cited section of the draft research's checks ran on and every section a later pass's checks ran on; failed, each section the final problems name), and the notice is in the step's words (`failureShare.ts`). When more than the share of at least `research.pass.failedStoriesMin` (5) stories failed technically, the run records the failed-stories notice (`stories-failing:research_pass`).
- **Owed stories** (`researchPassQueue.ts`, SELECT only): selected, no research applied (`pre_research` null), and no pipeline pass. A pipeline pass settles its story once it is completed, skipped or failed, or running past the pass's stale rule; an editor's pass settles nothing.
- **The publish gate** (`awaitingPass`, after research's and context's, each holding only while its own job is on): while the step works and its job is enabled, a selected story with no settled pipeline pass waits at most `research.pipeline.maxWaitMinutes` (120) after its selection, **in the daily edition too**. The cards are made inside the pass, so no separate wait for cards exists, and a card failure holds nothing.
- **One switch at a time** (`jobEnableChecks.ts`, `services/job.ts`): enabling `research_pass` switches `research_stories` and `build_story_context` off in the same transaction, and the scheduler reloads all three; enabling either old job is refused (422) while it is on. Switching back is the rollback: disable it, then enable the old two.

## How the switch stays off

Until the owner switches the step on (plan phase 6), four layers each keep production from running a pass:

1. **The eval gate** (`PASS_EVAL_GATE`, `services/researchPassGate.ts`): a constant, not a setting, so nothing at run time opens it; the change that switches the step on sets it to null. While it is set, enabling the job is refused (422, naming it), the step "does not work", and every editor's start is refused (409).
2. **The job row is disabled**: the chain skips a disabled step, and a step has no schedule of its own.
3. **The handler** researches nothing unless the gate is open, research's switch on and the job enabled.
4. **The publish gate** holds nothing for the step unless it works and its job is enabled.

So no pass row, version or Update exists: `updates` is empty on every story, and the old research and context steps run as before.

## Admin routes

`/api/admin/research-passes` (`routes/admin/researchPass.ts`, admins and editors; reads in `services/researchPassViews.ts`):

| Route | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| `GET /usage` | The month's spend under the one cap, both ceilings, whether the step is on (`on`: the eval gate open and the job enabled, the one thing the admin's buttons key on) and why no editor's pass can start now (`refusal`, null while one can) |
| `GET /stories/:storyId` | The story's passes, newest first (each row with its record, and by the pass's own rules its calls under the pass's step names, `passCalls`, and the problems its final drafts kept, `passProblems`, which the cost table and the checks show: `passRecordView`), its versions, newest first (reverted ones too), its pool (sources, facts, searches, the pages left out) and its legacy research runs; 404 for an unknown story |
| `POST /stories/:storyId/passes` | An editor's "Research again": 202 `{ taskId, passId }`; 409 while switched off, for a status other than analyzed, selected or published, a story without the analysis research needs, a pass running, or at the cap; 404 |
| `POST /passes` | The bulk "Research again" over `{ storyIds }`: 202 `{ taskId }`; 409 while switched off or when another task processes every one of them |
| `POST /stories/:storyId/undo` | "Undo this update": 204; 409 with nothing to undo, for a status that takes no research write, or when the story changed |

There is no apply endpoint: a pass applies itself. `passEligibility(db, storyId)` (`services/researchPassInput.ts`) gives a story's status and whether a pass of it is running. The kind of pass is the claim's alone (`passKindOf`, after the legacy import): `later` when the story has a current version to judge (a story with `stories.research` set gets version 1 from the import), else `first`, so a story whose first passes kept no article gets a first pass again.

## The public updates field

`GET /api/stories/{slug}` serves `updates` (`publicUpdateSchema`, `services/articleVersions.ts`; additive to `PublicStoryDetail`, snapshot refreshed): newest first, every version with a note that is not reverted (a later pass's update, or a first pass's Update on a published story), each with its day (UTC, `YYYY-MM-DD`), its note, each changed section shown on the page with its earlier text and whether the update removed it, labeled by where it was before (`from`; the summary and the blurb stay in the admin), and the sources the note and the earlier texts cite (found in the version, else in the version it replaced). As for `research`, an earlier story no longer published leaves with its citations and the rest take their current slug, in one lookup for both. `aiGenerated.fields` lists `updates` while the story serves any (`aiProvenance.ts`). An undone update is reverted, so it leaves the list.

## The cards a pass writes

A pass's cards are its `pass_boxes`; `services/storyCards.ts` (`cardRecords`) decides which cards a story has: **the newest pass's boxes when any pass wrote some, else the context runs' `context_boxes`** (`.context/story-context.md`), never a mix. A pass's cards cite the pool's facts by their refs (`cardFacts`, `researchPool.ts`), and a pool fact a later pass demoted is no longer shown (`shownFact`), so a card's sentence citing it goes, as one citing a context fact no longer shown does. The public route (`GET /api/stories/:slug/context`) keeps its shape, so the client's context components did not change; the admin's card list adds the pass that wrote the cards (`pass: { id, number, writtenAt }`, null for a context run's), named above them ("Research pass 3 wrote these cards on <date and time>.") in place of the context run's record. An editor's hide, hide all and move work on whichever table the card is in: hide all acts on the newest pass's boxes only, so an older pass's and the legacy boxes stay as they were. With no pass row, every story gets its context run's cards, as before.

## The admin

The Research tab of the story page (`/admin/stories/:id`, `StoryResearch.tsx`) and the action bars key on `GET /usage`'s `on`:

- **While the step is off**, nothing of it shows: the tab shows research's runs, the footer offers Research and Gather context, and the bulk bar Research and apply, as before. A story with passes or versions shows its pass history even then (a step switched off after it ran).
- **While it is on**, the server refuses the old tools whose writes would bypass the article's versions (`replacedByPasses`, `researchPassQueue.ts`, keyed on the same `on`, `passStepOn`): applying a research run, an editor's one or many and a research step's run still in flight (`applyRefusal`, `putResearch`: 409 for an editor, skipped for the step), and an editor's "Gather context" (`startContextGathering` 409, `gatherState`'s refusal). Starting a research run is not refused: it writes only its own run row, which can no longer be applied. Reverting to single-source never is.
- **While it is on**, the footer's Research and Gather context give way to **"Research again"** (`StoryActionBar`, `storyActions.ts`), which asks first only when the editor has unsaved edits ("Discard and research again"), runs as a polled `research` task and refreshes the story, its passes and its cards; refusals come with their reasons as other actions' do. The bulk bar's Research and apply give way to "Research again" over the selection, confirmed with its bound ("Research 12 stories again?"; at most each story at the larger ceiling).
- **The tab** (`StoryPasses`, `ResearchPassView.tsx`): a line on how a pass applies itself with the month's spend under the cap and both reservations; why none can start, when that is so; "Revert to single-source" and **"Undo this update"** (`UndoUpdate`, `ResearchVersion.tsx`), shown only while the current version is an update and confirmed with the version it goes back to; a picker over the passes, newest first, then the legacy research runs (read-only: no "Use this version"); the picked pass's record (status, outcome, what changed: a first pass's draft as research's run view shows it, a later pass's judgments, each changed section before and after, and the Update note; the searches made and skipped; the sources new this pass; the facts new, checked, rejected, demoted with their reasons and superseded; the cards; the checks; the cost by step); the article's versions; and the story's evidence pool.

## The story page

A researched story's page shows its Updates (ADR-0068, "Where Updates show"; `.context/public-website.md`, "Updates"): "Updated <date>" in the header row, the newest Update's day, linking the Updates section after Caveats (`StoryUpdates.tsx`; each Update's day and note with its citations, and per section it changed the earlier text in a closed native disclosure with the citations it carried); an "Updated" link after every paragraph and point an Update rewrote (`updateMarks.ts`, replayed oldest first so a removal moves later marks up; the headline and key quote carry none); and `article:modified_time` with the NewsArticle's `dateModified` at the newest Update's day. The Updates show only with the cited version they changed, and a story with none renders as before.

## Evals and the gate

`eval:research` and `eval:overviews --steps context` run the first pass through one adapter (`scripts/eval/research/pipeline.ts`; `.context/model-eval.md`): eval:research reads it as research's record, with the card steps' calls and credits counted; the `context` step replays it on the gap keys' stories (`loadFirstPassInput`), judges its cards, and holds the whole pass to $0.20 a story at flex. `eval:overviews --steps update` replays the later pass's article step on the answer keys' researched stories with the facts found since and planted corrections, and holds it to ADR-0068's bars. The step stays off until the plan's bars hold on the judged half; the owner switches it on.
